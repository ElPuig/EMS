# What's new:

## Configurable family notification for strikes:
- New "Family notification" setting under Settings → EMS Management → "Strikes Settings": choose whether the family (minors, or adults who authorized sharing information) is notified on every strike, or only when the student was kicked out of class. The student and the group tutor are always notified either way.
- New installations start on "Kicked out only"; an installation upgrading from an earlier version keeps today's "All strikes" behaviour unchanged.
