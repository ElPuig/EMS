# Fixes

## Tutors can delete their students' family contacts:

- A tutor got an "Access error" (no delete rights on Partner relation) when removing a family contact from their own student's Contacts & Addresses tab; adding one already worked.
- Tutors can now delete and edit the relations between their own tutees and their family contacts, and nobody else's. Secretary, Head of Studies and admin keep their centre-wide access.
- Deleting a family contact from the student form also removes that contact itself when it is left without any related student and has no user (portal access); otherwise it is kept.
