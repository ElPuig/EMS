# Changes

## Tutor permissions escalate along the chain of command (Seminar/Department Chief, Head of Studies, Director):

- Chiefs inherit the tutor group, but every tutor-scoped record rule and check compared the student's `tutor_id.user_id` with the current user, and chiefs usually tutor no group themselves. In practice a Head of Studies was left with a plain teacher's rights on everything tutor-scoped: found with the Google credentials of issue #478 (Documentation tab and the "Download Google credentials" action showed nothing).
- New non-stored, searchable field `hr.employee.tutor_scope_user_ids` (on the shared employee base, so also on `hr.employee.public`): the employee's own user, every ancestor through `parent_id` (the department cascade) whose user is in `group_department_chief` (Seminar Chief, Department Chief, Head of Studies/Deputy and Director all are), and the company's Director. All 18 tutor-scoped record rules (`security/rules/{contacts,attendance,coexistence,grading}.xml`) now match on `tutor_id.tutor_scope_user_ids` instead of `tutor_id.user_id`, and the Python tutor checks (student tutor/read-only checks, justification checks, grade session `can_edit` in board state, portal access / graduation / EM grading wizards, authorization send wizard's student filter) go through a new shared `ems.base.user_acts_as_tutor()`. Any future tutor filter written the same way inherits the hierarchy.
- Scoped to the real chain, as the "escalate by hierarchy, not by role" rule from the v18.0.0.26.1 `CLAUDE.md` asks: only the tutor's own chiefs get the tutor's rights, never another department's chief or another area's Head of Studies; the Director covers every student with a tutor. Not stored, so a change of department, chief, area manager or Director applies immediately.
- Permissions only: "My students" (default facet), the tutor enrollment list and the authorization follow-up screen still list only the user's own groups, and strike/attendance notifications still go only to the literal tutor. The EM grading wizard's group picker and the attendance justification's student picker do follow the hierarchy, so they offer exactly what the server then accepts; creating justifications is open to any user who acts as tutor of some group.
- An intermediate, centre-wide read rule for Head of Studies on Google credentials (`rule_ems_student_document_head_of_studies`, pushed earlier on this same branch) was removed in favour of this mechanism.
- `CLAUDE.md`'s hierarchy rule and `plans/permission_hierarchy_escalation.md` now point to this mechanism as the implemented part (tutor-scoped rights); the audit of flat, non-tutor rules stays pending there.
- Covered by new `TestTutorScope` (compute/search consistency, seminar chief in the chain, out-of-scope chief and Head of Studies, Director, hierarchy change applied at once, a record rule, the wizard checks and both pickers), the rewritten `TestStudentDocumentHeadOfStudiesAccess`, and the tutor credentials tour run as the Head of Studies above the tutor. New shared fixture `create_head_of_studies_branch()` in `tests/common.py`. Developer doc (`role_hierarchy.md` "Tutor scope" section, `student_document.md`), admin roles manual, Head of Studies index and tutors' credentials manual (ca/es/en), and ca/es translations updated.
