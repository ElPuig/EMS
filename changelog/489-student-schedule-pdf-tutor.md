# What's new

## Student schedule PDF shows the tutor and their email:

- The student's schedule PDF (from the student form's Print menu and from the family portal's Download PDF) now shows, under the group, the group tutor's name followed by their work email as a clickable `mailto:` link, so families know who to contact.
- Also fixed the Spanish translation of the "Tutor:" label, which was missing its colon.
