# What's new

## Personal emails can no longer be corporate addresses:
- A student's, applicant's, ex-student's or family member's personal email (res.partner.email, for every contact type in PERSONAL_EMAIL_CONTACT_TYPES) and an employee's private_email are rejected with a ValidationError when they belong to the centre's own Google Workspace domain (res.company.google_ws_domain) or any subdomain of it. Staff work contacts (no contact_type) and providers are not checked, since their email is the corporate account itself.
- Why: the personal email is where Google Workspace credentials are delivered and the account's recovery address, so a corporate address there makes both useless.
- Enforced at ORM level (@api.constrains on email / private_email), so the contact form, "My Profile", the portal access wizard, merges and imports are all covered. Only triggered by the email field itself, not by contact_type, so a legacy corporate value never blocks an enrollment/graduation type change. Existing data is not migrated.
- Skipped on development databases (ems.environment_type = 'dev'), because devel.sh rewrites every stored address onto the corporate domain; a database with no declared environment (e.g. CI) is checked.
- The three bulk imports (Esfera student import, including tutor/family emails; CSV student update; GEDAC applicant import) no longer fail the row on a corporate address: the value is dropped (the current personal email is kept) and a warning is listed in the result. The CSV update and GEDAC wizards gained a "Warnings" block in their result for this.
- Help tooltips on the student/applicant personal email and the employee private email (form and "My Profile") explain the rule.
- Shared helpers on res.company: _ems_is_corporate_email(), _ems_check_personal_email(), _ems_drop_corporate_email(); test helper enforce_corporate_email_policy() in tests/common.py. New tests in test_personal_email_not_corporate.py, the three import wizard test files and a secretary tour on the student form. Catalan/Spanish translations, developer docs and the secretary/admin/head-of-studies manuals updated.
