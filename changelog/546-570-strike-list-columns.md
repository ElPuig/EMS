# What's new

## Strikes show the group, subject and classroom:

- Every strike now records the student's group, and the subject and classroom of the session
  where it was issued (issues #546, #570). They are frozen when the strike is issued: a later
  group change or session edit never rewrites an old strike.
- A strike issued outside class (New strike) takes the student's main group, with no subject
  or classroom.
- Coexistence > Strikes shows a Group column, plus Subject and Classroom as optional columns;
  the strike's form shows them too. Existing strikes are filled in on upgrade.
