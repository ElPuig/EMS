# What's new

## Student's IDALU on the convalidation request:

- The convalidation request form shows the student's IDALU (under *Requested by*), with a
  one-click copy button, so the Head of Studies can paste it into Esfera to look up the
  student's academic record without opening the student's own form.
- The convalidation list has an optional IDALU column (hidden by default), and the search
  bar's *Student* field also matches the IDALU.
