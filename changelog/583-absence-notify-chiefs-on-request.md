# Changes

## Absence requests reach the department's chiefs when they are filed:

- When an employee sends an absence request, their Department Chief and, when the department
  has one, their Seminar Chief now receive an email with the summary (employee, absence type,
  dates, hours, status), without the written reason. Until now the chief only heard of it once
  it was approved or refused, too late to plan the department's cover.
- Both chiefs follow the request from then on, so they also receive anything posted in its
  chatter. The approver is not sent this summary (they already have the approval activity).
- The Seminar Chief is also informed of the outcome, like the Department Chief already was.
