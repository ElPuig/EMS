# What's new

## Teacher vacancies pending identification, created from the form (#584):

- The teacher form has a new "Staffing type" selector: "Named teacher" (default) or "Vacancy
  pending identification". A vacancy asks for a vacancy code (e.g. X1) instead of a personal
  email, and gets no Google account and no EMS user while it stays pending: until now the form
  required a personal email, so a post created by hand got a Google account under the post's
  name (found in production with a post "X1" in September).
- The selector has no column of its own: it reads and writes `schedule_import_code`, the field
  the schedule importer already sets on its placeholders, so imported and hand-made vacancies are
  the same thing and need no migration.
- Identifying the person is switching the selector to "Named teacher" with their real name and
  personal email: the code is cleared with a chatter note, and the same save queues the automatic
  Google account creation (#582); the form requires the personal email for that switch. The
  "Mark as identified" action is removed. A person who
  already has a corporate account is linked with "Assign corporate email manually" and "Create
  EMS User", which confirms the identity the same way.
- `_gw_ready()` never holds for a vacancy, `_gw_create_account()` refuses one, and no
  "missing data" note is posted on it.
- Vacancy codes are unique among active employees, stored stripped and compared ignoring case;
  the schedule importer looks them up the same way, so a vacancy created on the form receives the
  schedule of a file naming its code.
- Manuals (head of studies, admin teacher onboarding and working schedules; ca/es/en) and the
  developer docs describe creating and identifying a vacancy; the head of studies manual has two
  new screenshots of the staffing type (generated by `test_docs_screenshots_head_of_studies.py`).

# Fixes

## Teacher name required again on the form (#584):

- EMS replaced the native name field of the employee form (to show a teacher/ASP placeholder) and
  lost its required attribute, so a teacher could be saved without a name and the database
  answered with an unreadable "resource.resource: Name" error (found by the Deputy Head of Studies
  creating a vacancy). The name is required again, so the form marks it in red before saving. A
  vacancy may leave it blank and takes its code as name ("X1"), free of language or gender; the
  schedule importer now names its placeholders the same way instead of "Pending teacher (X1)".
