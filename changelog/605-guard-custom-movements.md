# What's new:

## Internal arrangements instead of a guard, detected by the board (guard duty board):
- For every class still left without a teacher, the board now works out how the group's own teachers that day could rearrange their lessons so that no guard is needed, and proposes it to whoever organises the absence (the absent teacher's chain of command, or the administrator): a "Possible arrangements" box with an "Apply" button, a tag on the absence row, and the options at the top of the class dialog. New `ems.course._get_arrangement_options()`.
- Two kinds are detected (developer's decision, 2026-10-09: only within the group and the day, all of them shown, ordered so the students lose as few lessons as possible): a lesson swap (another teacher of the group, free now, teaches now and the absent teacher teaches that teacher's other lesson later; nobody loses a lesson), and a moved lesson (another teacher brings their lesson from the start or end of the group's day, or next to a break, into the empty one; the group comes in later, leaves earlier or has a longer break). A moved lesson that nobody was going to teach anyway costs nothing and is ranked with the swaps.
- No arrangement makes anybody's working day longer (developer's decision, 2026-10-10): a teacher who would come in earlier must leave earlier, and the other way round; options that would lengthen a teacher's day (from their first to their last period, guards and other duties included) are not offered. Any change to a teacher's entry or exit time is spelt out in the proposal, tagged "Changes entry/exit", and in the teachers' notification; those options come after the ones that change nobody's hours. Lessons shared with another group are never moved or swapped.
- Anything the board cannot work out (two groups joined for the same subject, a teacher of another group...) can still be recorded by hand from the dialog, with the teacher involved if any, so that it is on record.
- Accepting a moved lesson and telling the families go together (developer's decision): applying it creates the draft notice for the change and opens it, and the arrangement only counts once the notice is sent (it shows among the proposals until then; deleting the draft discards it). Notice creation extracted into `ems.notice._create_absence_change_notice()`, shared with `board_propose_absence_change()`.
- The teachers involved are notified when an arrangement takes effect and when it is withdrawn, like a guard (developer's decision): for a moved lesson, when its notice is sent.
- Nobody looks free when they are not: a teacher with a group through an arrangement is struck out of the guard column in that period (and, in a swap, the absent teacher at the lesson they take over), never offered as a guard then, and the row cannot be taken by a guard on duty. The timetable tab and the PDF show who really teaches each affected lesson.
- An arrangement that no longer applies (the absence changed, or a teacher it relies on becomes absent) appears under "Guards no longer needed" with "Remove arrangement". New fields on `ems.absence_cover`: `cover_type` (guard/arrangement, default guard, no migration needed), `arrangement_type` (swap/move/manual), `vacated_hour_from/to`, `notice_id`, non-stored `is_effective`; new `board_arrange()`.
- Backend tests in TestAbsenceCoverage (detection and ordering, swap, guard who swaps, moved lesson with its notice, edges only, manual, validations, replacement both ways, obsolete arrangements, visibility by role, PDF); new tour `ems_absence_arrangement` as the Department Chief; ca/es translations; developer doc updated.
