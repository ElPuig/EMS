# What's new:

## Internal arrangements instead of a guard (guard duty board):
- From the Absences table, whoever organises an absence (the absent teacher's chain of command, or the administrator) can now record that a class needs no guard because the teachers rearranged themselves (two teachers swap lessons, one moves a lesson forward so the group leaves earlier...). The class assignment dialog has a second option, "No guard needed: internal arrangement": who is with the group instead, a free-text description of the movement (required), and optionally which of that teacher's lessons with the group is left free. New `ems.absence_cover.cover_type` (`guard`/`arrangement`, default `guard`, no migration needed) plus `vacated_hour_from/to`, `notice_id` and the non-stored `is_effective`; new `board_arrange()` and `ems.course.get_arrangement_candidates()` (the group's own teachers first, then whoever is on guard duty).
- Accepting an arrangement and telling the families go together (developer's decision, 2026-10-09): when the lesson left free lets the group come in later, leave earlier or have a longer break, saving creates the draft notice for that change and opens it, and the arrangement only counts once the notice is sent (it shows among the proposals until then; deleting the draft discards it). A free lesson in the middle of the day is refused (a guard covers that). Notice creation extracted into `ems.notice._create_absence_change_notice()`, shared with `board_propose_absence_change()`.
- Nobody can be taken for free when they are not: a teacher with a group through an arrangement is struck out of the guard column in that period (screen, both tabs, and PDF, with the reason as tooltip), is never offered as a guard then, and the row cannot be taken by a guard on duty. The row itself is struck out with the teacher who is with the group and the description.
- An arrangement that no longer applies (the absence was refused or shrank, or the teacher it relies on becomes absent) appears under "Guards no longer needed" with "Remove arrangement"; removing it or replacing it with a guard notifies nobody, while replacing a guard with an arrangement releases and notifies that guard.
- Backend tests in TestAbsenceCoverage (swap, busy guard, early leave with notice, draft deletion, middle-of-the-day refusal, validations, replacement both ways, obsolete arrangements, candidates, PDF); new tour `ems_absence_arrangement` as the Department Chief; ca/es translations; developer doc updated.
