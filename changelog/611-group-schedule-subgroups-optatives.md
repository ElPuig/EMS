# What's new

## A group's schedule includes the classes of its subgroups and optatives:

- A split half, a flexible group or an optative is a group of its own, so until now the main group's schedule (and its PDF and public link) had a hole wherever its students were split. A reinforcement group can now name its main groups (new **Main groups** field on its form, `ems.group.parent_group_ids`), and its classes are then shown in the schedule of each of them, labelled with the subgroup.
- Up to 3 classes at the same time are shown side by side. With 4 or more (e.g. all the optatives of a course) a single "N classes in parallel" block is shown, and a "Classes in parallel" table under the grid (and in the PDF) lists the subgroup, subject, teacher and classroom of each.
- A change to a subgroup's classes, or to its main groups, refreshes the public PDF of the main groups too.
- New browser tour for the group's Schedule tab with subgroups, run as a plain teacher.

## Física i Química of 1r Batxillerat:

- New BTX subject `FIQ1` Física i Química (1r), the 4-hour modality subject the 2026-27 timetable needs. `FIS1` and `QUI1` stay as they are (iEduca's Ampliació de Física / Ampliació de Química map to them).
