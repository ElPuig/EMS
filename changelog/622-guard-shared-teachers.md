# What's new:
## Absences of shared teachers can be organised by the department of the group (issue #622):
- Who organises an absence from the guard duty board (sending, changing or releasing a guard, internal arrangements, late entry / early leave notices) no longer depends only on the absent teacher's department: whoever answers for the students who lose the class can do it too. A class of a group whose study names a managing department can be organised by that department's Department Chief and Seminar Chief and whoever is above them (area manager, Direction). A Foreign Languages teacher's lesson with a Computer Science group can be covered by the Computer Science chiefs; her lesson with an Administration group, by the Administration ones.
- Purely additive: the absent teacher's own chain of command keeps everything it could do before. Restricting who may act (e.g. only the Head of Studies in ESO/BTX) is left to issue #602.
- New field "Managed by the department" on the study (`ems.study.managing_department_id`), edited by the administrator; the group form shows the resulting department(s) read-only (`ems.group.managing_department_ids`). A reinforcement group, which has no study, follows the studies of its main groups. A study with no department (ESO/BTX, whose departments are by subject) adds nobody.
- New field "Also managed by the departments" on the employee form, below Manager (`hr.employee.managing_department_ids`): for a teacher who in practice works for another department or area than the one they belong to. The chiefs of those departments, and whoever is above them, can organise everything of that teacher, including their fixed guard duties (toilets, break), which have no group. It does not change the Manager or who approves their absences.
- Expected absences: a chief can also enter one for a teacher of another department who teaches a group their department manages, or who names their department as also managing them.
- Department and Seminar Chiefs can send notices to the groups their department manages, besides those it teaches.
- Migration 18.0.0.37.0 (and the clean-install hook) sets each study's managing department to the one whose teachers give more than half of its groups' classes; studies where no department reaches half stay blank. Check the result on Educational Community > Configuration > Curriculum > Studies after deploying. "Also managed by the departments" starts empty for everyone.

# Internal changes:
## Department chiefs resolved in one place:
- `hr.department._chief_users()` walks a department's Department/Seminar Chiefs up its parent chain; the notices' "groups their department teaches" scope and the new managing scopes all use it.
