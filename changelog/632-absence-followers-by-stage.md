# Changes

## Staff absences: the conversation on a request follows its stage (issue #632):

- A request is now followed by the employee and whoever has it in hand, and nobody else: the Head (Head of Studies, Deputy Head of Studies or Secretary, i.e. the employee's Area Manager) from its filing until they validate it and pass it on to Direction, the Director from then on. A message written on the request ("Send message") therefore reaches only the other side of the conversation. Before, the Department Chief, the Seminar Chief and the Director followed every request from the start and received every message written on it.
- The followers are synchronised on every status change (`_ems_sync_followers()`, called from `_ems_update_activities()`). Only the Head and the Director change hands; anybody else following the request, or who wrote on it, is left alone. A refused or cancelled request keeps whoever had it when it was closed.
- Who has it in hand (`_ems_stage_users()`): the Head while it is Pending, Awaiting documentation, Pending validation, and Pending ATRI confirmation after their validation; the Director once it is Pending Direction or Approved, and when Direction sends an ATRI absence back (it is settled on the ATRI portal and comes straight back to Direction). A supporting document Direction finds insufficient is validated by the Head again, so that request goes back to the Head. With no Director (none configured, or the Director is the absent employee) the Head keeps it throughout.
- Whoever newly takes the request receives its summary with "It is now in your hands.", unless they moved it there themselves; the activity of that same step is then scheduled without Odoo's own email, so it is one email, not two. Returning to Pending needs no such note: the approval activity's email already says it.
- The Director is now told when the request reaches Direction, not at the Head's first acknowledgement while the supporting document is still due.
- The decision summary (`_ems_post_outcome()`) is now a note to the employee alone, instead of a comment to every follower.
- The Department Chief and the Seminar Chief no longer follow requests. They still receive the filing, and afterwards only what changes the guard-duty cover they arranged (`_ems_inform_department()`): the absence will not take place (refused, cancelled, or deleted while still pending, through `message_notify` since the request is gone), goes ahead again (a refusal or cancellation undone), or its dates or type changed.
- The escalation of an employee's overdue step goes to whoever has the request in hand: the Director for an ATRI absence it sent back, instead of the Head.
- `_ems_return_atri()` now records Direction's decision before changing the status, so the request is handed on knowing the decision was Direction's.
- Upgrade: `migrations/18.0.0.37.0/post-migrate.py` resets the followers of every existing request (`_ems_reset_followers()`): the Department Chief and the Seminar Chief stop following them unless they have them in hand, and every open request gets the followers its stage gives it. Nobody is told. On this box's dev DB it changed 31 of 33 requests (61 subscriptions removed, 10 added), with no message created.
- Developer doc (`docs/en/developers/employees/absence.md`, "Who gets told") and the teachers' and Head of Studies' absence manuals (ca/es/en) describe the new behaviour; new strings translated to Catalan and Spanish.
