# Changes

## Guard arrangements send one notice per group, straight away:

- Accepting a guard duty board proposal (issue #652) now creates one single notice per group and day for its families, telling every change the proposal brings to that day (a later entry, an earlier leave, a longer break) in one email, instead of one draft per change. Each notice stays addressed to its own group only; linked groups never share one.
- The notices are sent as soon as the proposal is accepted: checking a notice step is the confirmation, there is no draft to open. The moved lessons waiting for the notice are in effect at once, and each teacher involved receives one Odoo message (no longer one per cover of the same move or per notice sent).
- The notice to the students about lessons that change time (issue #649) is sent at once too when a proposal or a swap is accepted. Correction drafts prepared when an arrangement is withdrawn or released, and single changes proposed with "Propose notice", stay drafts to review as before.
- If a notice cannot be sent (no recipients, a sender without an email address...), it stays a draft and the board opens it with the reason; the moves waiting for it keep showing as "waiting for their notice" with "Open draft" until it is sent from its form. A draft already prepared for one of the same changes is replaced by the new notice.
- Technical: the single-change fields of a timetable-change notice (`absence_change_type`, `absence_change_hour`, `absence_change_hour_to`) are replaced by a JSON list, `ems.notice.absence_changes`, read by every board reader (`_get_absence_change_states()` and the rectification, strike-out and "Open draft" logic); the form shows it as one line (`absence_change_label`). Migration `18.0.0.39.0/post-migrate.py` moves each existing notice's change into a one-item list (the manifest version is not bumped in this branch). New helpers: `ems.notice._send_or_keep_draft()`, `ems.absence_cover._board_send_notices()`, `_board_arrange_cover()` and `_notify_arrangements()`.
