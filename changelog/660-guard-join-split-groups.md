# What's new

## Guard duty board: join the two halves of a split group (issue #660):

- New kind of internal arrangement, "Joined groups": when a teacher is away, the teacher who teaches the
  same subject to the other half of a split group at that time can teach both halves together, bringing
  the lesson they had with this half from another hour. That lesson is left free like a moved lesson's,
  so the group can leave earlier or come in later instead of needing a guard (typically both halves
  leave an hour earlier).
- Halves are deduced from the weekly timetable, with no configuration: two groups sharing some lesson.
  Only the same subject is joined, and nobody's working day gets longer.
- The planner proposes it whenever it improves the day, combined with notices and other moves; whoever
  manages the board decides whether to apply it, and can withdraw it like any other arrangement.
- Notices as with moved lessons: the families of each half whose day changes (entry or exit), the
  students of the half that moves (with the other half's name and the room) and the teacher.
- The timetable and its PDF show "Together with ..." on both halves.
- Planner and board tests, a browser tour, user manuals (ca/es/en), developer doc and translations.
