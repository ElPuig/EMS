# Fixes

## Guard duty board: a guard's badge is coloured only where they cover a class:
- The pairing colour of a covered class was given to the guard's badge in every block of their guard duty that shift, so a guard on duty 10-12 sent to a 10-11 class showed coloured from 10 to 12 too. Now the badge in the Guard duty column (screen and PDF) only takes the colour, and the dotted "partial" border, in the blocks overlapping what they actually cover; the absence rows keep the shift-wide colour, so a cover on another line than the guard's block still matches. A block where the guard covers nothing no longer reserves a colour either, so the palette is shared out a bit better.
