# What's new

## Erasmus+ scoring and interviews (phase 2 of #682):

- Every application of a call gets the score of art. 12 of the call: A record average x 4, B interview (general 20 + English 15), C language certificate, D fewer opportunities, E first mobility, F -3 per conduct warning (max -9). A criterion left empty keeps the score incomplete instead of counting as zero, and every manual criterion records who set it and when.
- Closing the call (*Close applications*) freezes, per submitted application, the record average and the conduct warnings EMS has at that moment. The average is computed by EMS: every module of the student's current cycle, all its years (past years' final grades, this year's provisional grade from the evaluated learning outcomes), one grade per module (the latest), convalidated/recognised modules and modules with nothing evaluated left out, weighted by module hours; the modules used are kept on the application (`ems.erasmus.application.grade`). Warnings: the student's strikes in groups of the same study up to the closing date. Applicants with no grades in EMS get the average of their certificate typed by the Erasmus team, the Secretariat or the Head of Studies.
- New interviews (`ems.erasmus.interview`): the team sets date and place, assigns two evaluators (any staff member) and emails the interview call (at least 24 hours ahead; also shown on the portal). Each evaluator scores annex 4's 7-criterion rubric (0-5) from the new *Erasmus+ > My evaluations*, gets an EMS task for it, and only sees their own interviews. They can reopen until the call's interview review deadline; afterwards only the team can, with a rectification note. The interview score is the mean of both evaluators, or one with a recorded reason.
- Ranking per modality (*Erasmus+ > Ranking*, and a button on the closed call): computed only once every admitted application of the modality has a complete score, with the tie-breaks of the call (fewer opportunities, first mobility, English part, record average) and a recorded draw for any remaining tie; applications under the call's minimum score (50 by default) are flagged as not eligible.
- Not attending the interview: *Exclude* now also works on admitted applications.
- New hidden group `ems.group_erasmus_evaluator`, added and removed by EMS as interviews are assigned; the Secretariat now opens Erasmus+ applications (read, and the typed average only).
- Manuals: scoring and interviews for the Erasmus team, scoring an interview for teachers, typing a record average for the Secretariat (also linked from the Head of Studies manual), and the interview call in the students' manual; in Catalan, Spanish and English. Developer doc updated.
