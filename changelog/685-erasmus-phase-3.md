# What's new

## Erasmus+ resolutions, claims and place offers (phase 3 of #682):

- New resolutions (`ems.erasmus.resolution`): admission list, provisional, definitive and any number of rectifications, each needing the previous one published. *Prepare lines* freezes the list as the applications are (admitted/excluded, or rank, every criterion, total and result per modality) and refuses while something is missing (an undecided application, an incomplete score or pending draw, an identity document). Publishing needs the Erasmus+ Commission's approval date and minutes; a published resolution can never be changed or deleted, a correction is a rectification.
- Results: selected within the modality's places, reserve after them, not selected under the minimum score. Publishing emails every applicant their result (with the claims deadline for the provisional one, the acceptance deadline if selected); the provisional one locks every interview of the call; the definitive one (or a rectification) sets each application's result and offers the place to the selected. A rectification never undoes an offer already answered.
- Published lists never show names: only the rank, four digits of the identity document (DNI `***4567**`, NIE `****4567*`, Spanish data protection agency's rule), the total and the result. Applicants see them on the portal and download them as PDF; the team prints the same list for the centre's website.
- Portal: a *Results* card on the application with each published result, the rank and every criterion; the place offer to accept or renounce (with an optional reason) before its deadline.
- Place offers (`ems.erasmus.offer`): no answer by the deadline is a renunciation (hourly cron). A freed place is never offered on its own: the call shows the free places and the next reserve per modality, and *Offer a free place* lets the team confirm it with a new deadline (emailed to the student). The team can withdraw an offer still waiting.
- Claims and appeals (`ems.erasmus.claim`): recorded by the team (claims arrive by replying to the result email), with the resolution they are against, the response and the resolution date.
- *Export to spreadsheet* (applications list, and the Actions of an accepted application and of the call): an `.xlsx` with one row per person who accepted a place, sheet and columns fixed for the external tool that imports it (personal data, cycle and year, bank account, and every legal guardian of a minor). Built on the fly, never stored, Erasmus team only.
- New call fields: claims deadline and acceptance deadline. New application states: selected, reserve, not selected, place accepted, renounced. New menus *Erasmus+ > Resolutions*, *Place offers* and *Claims* (read only for the Head of Studies and the Director).
- Manuals: resolutions, claims and place offers for the Erasmus team, and results and accepting the place in the students' manual; in Catalan, Spanish and English. Developer doc updated.
