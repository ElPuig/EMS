# Changes:

## Detected absences reviewed by the Head of Studies first (issue #707):
- Every detected absence (missing check-in or informed by hand, developer's decision: informed ones too, even when the reviewer entered them) starts "To review": the teacher is not reminded, nothing is reported, and the teacher cannot see it (record rules hide their own `pending` entries; the teacher picker never offers the user themselves).
- On creation, a "Review detected absence" activity (`ems.mail_activity_absence_pending_review`) goes to the teacher's attendance approvers (`hr.employee._find_attendance_approvers()`, never the teacher themselves; the administrators as fallback). No email of its own: it shows in the activities and the daily pending-tasks digest. Marked done as soon as the entry leaves "To review", whichever way.
- Four actions, in the form's Actions dropdown and as list bulk actions (server actions + an act_window for the wizard, list only), checked server-side per record (`_ems_review`): on a mixed selection the eligible ones are processed and the user is told how many were skipped and why.
- Request justification: new state `justification_requested`; the existing reminders/report start, both delays now counted from `justification_date` instead of the absence's day.
- Correct check-in: creates closed `hr.attendance` records for the gaps of the span no attendance covers (the teacher's own late check-in is kept, not moved), with `ems_checked_in_by_id` = the reviewer and a chatter note; nobody is notified; new state `corrected`. Only once the absence is over.
- Create absence: wizard `ems.absence_pending.leave_wizard` (one absence type for the whole selection), one `hr.leave` per entry with the detected day/hours, filed with the new `hr.leave.ems_filed_by_id` (waives the responsible declaration, which the teacher never made). Acknowledged by the reviewer when they may approve that teacher's absences (or are an administrator); otherwise left submitted for its usual approver (developer's decision). The teacher follows it and gets a note.
- Discard: `discarded` + archived (new `active` field), with a note naming who; a discard by a later check-in keeps the system's note and stays active.
- Who may review: `hr.employee._ems_is_attendance_approver()`, extracted from `ems.attendance_correction._check_is_approver` and shared by both (technical administrators now included too). Department and Seminar Chiefs keep reading and entering entries, but cannot review.
- The guard duty board, the teacher's own absence linking and the later check-in review treat `pending` and `justification_requested` alike (still unresolved).
- Academic administrators can now read and manage detected absences (ACL, rule, menu): they already received the "not requested" report activity, and now the review task, for teachers with no Head of Studies above them, on a record they could not open.
- Migration 18.0.0.41.0 (post-migrate): entries already reminded or reported move to `justification_requested` (with `justification_date` = the absence's last day, so their reminders carry on as before); the rest stay "To review" and get their review task. No clean-install counterpart needed (no detected absences on a new database).
- Docs: developer doc (state diagram, actions, access table); Head of Studies, teacher, admin and guard duty schedule manuals in ca/es/en; two screenshots refreshed.
