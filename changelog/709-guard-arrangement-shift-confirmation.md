# Fixes

## Guard duty arrangements no longer change a teacher's entry or exit time when they swap a lesson with a duty:
- When an arrangement takes a teacher's lesson to an hour where they have a guard, a coordination or any other non-teaching duty, the duty now swaps to the hour the lesson leaves (`schedule_shift()`'s new `duties`, from `ems.course._duty_periods()`): the teacher's day is unchanged, so the proposal no longer says they come in later or leave earlier, and the ranking no longer counts it as a change. Only a lesson taken to an hour off their timetable changes their entry or exit time.
- A swapped regular guard moves on the board too (`_moved_guards()`, also giving #678's co-teachers left on guard): once the arrangement is in effect the teacher is struck out at the guard's hour and on guard at the lesson's usual hour, tagged "guard moved from HH:MM", in the guard column, the PDF and the guard candidates. Meetings, coordinations and other duties show nowhere.
- Proposal steps now read as one short line ("Ferran: 13:00 lesson → 10:00 (AIX1A and AIX1B)", "Sergi and Carla swap their 08:00 and 11:00 lessons (AIX1A)") with a grey line under it saying what kind of move it is for each teacher: a swap with their guard (which moves to the lesson's hour) or another non-teaching hour, a free hour, a shifted working day, or groups joined. Moves to a free hour are still proposed, with the confirmation below.
- A teacher's timetable cannot be changed without them: applying steps that change somebody's entry or exit time now asks first ("Working day changes: ... Have you talked to ...?"). "Not yet" applies nothing and keeps the dialog the plan came from open.
- Tests: `schedule_shift` unit test, moved guard and swapped meeting on the board, updated chain assertions, the arrangement tours confirm (and once cancel) the new dialog. Teachers' guard-duty manual (ca/es/en) and developer doc updated, ca/es translations added. The chained-arrangement screenshot test now switches the timetable-change settings on itself and captures the new dialog and the moved guard.
