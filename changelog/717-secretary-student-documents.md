# What's new

## Secretary's office can add student documents sent by email:

- When a family can't upload a document from the portal and emails it instead (ID card, medical card, IBAN, proof of a benefit...), the secretary's office and the academic administration can now add it from the Documentation section of the student's Secretary tab ("Add a line"), or with New on Academic Management > Student Documents.
- A document added this way is approved on the spot with the same effects as approving it (an IBAN becomes the active, trusted bank account; a benefit document creates the student benefit; the previous approved document of that type is replaced), but without a review task and without emailing the family: the approval is logged as an internal note.
- Its type, file and data can only be filled in before the first save; the student is fixed when added from the student's own form. Tutors, TAC and Head of Studies keep the section read only.

# Changes

## Student document form: actions in the "Actions" dropdown:

- Approve, Reject and Reset to pending moved from loose header buttons into the form's Actions dropdown, and the "Cancel submission" action the secretary manual already described (but the form never offered) is now there too.
