# What's new

## Previous curriculum records from the grades certificate (Certificat de qualificacions):

- "Add a previous curriculum record" now also reads the "Certificat de qualificacions de mòduls i unitats formatives" PDF, besides the "Historial acadèmic": same review grid with every module and unit, hours, grade and sitting.
- The reader accepts letter codes for a centre's own modules and units (e.g. AOC / AOCOA), and the end of a certificate's page (no signatures, only its footer) no longer runs into the next page's header.
- The certificate has no IDALU: the student is checked against their Document ID (DNI/NIE), ignoring spaces, dots, hyphens and case; a different one, or a student without one, blocks the import.
- A certificate never proposes "Title obtained", since it may list only some of the cycle's modules. The document field is now called "Academic document"; manuals (ca/es/en) and screenshots updated.
