# Changes

## Erasmus+ polish: texts and names:

- Catalan and Spanish labels renamed as the review of 46.0 agreed: "Comissió" instead of "Tribunal" everywhere (screens, messages, manuals), "Enviada", "Alumnat", one name for conduct warnings ("Amonestacions"), "Puntuació fixada el", "Resultat" and "Situació actual" in the ranking, "Poden sol·licitar", "Termini de valoració de la comissió" (staff calls only), "Empatats", "Places de docents", "Inscrits", "Signatures", "Equip Erasmus", the commitment letter's "Signada el" and "Per". English sources stay, except where a label had to read differently.
- Short list headers (`string=` on the list field), so that forms keep their full label: calls, mobilities, ranking, group mobilities, economic panel, grant payments, diffusion actions, earlier courses, rates.
- Staff application: the correction fields are called "Correcció" and read "Correcte, com ha declarat" while empty; the commission's ratings have a title and no repeated evaluator.
- Points show their maximum ("36 / 40") with new widget `ems_score_out_of`.
- A complete document offers "Reobrir" instead of "Cal corregir" (the same button).
- Earlier courses: the list hides the source and a line opens in a form that shows it whole. "Descarrega l'arxiu del projecte (ZIP)" and "Descarrega l'economia (Excel)" say what they give.
