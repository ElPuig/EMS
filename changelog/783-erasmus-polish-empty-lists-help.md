# Changes

## Erasmus+ empty lists and help texts (issue #783):
- Empty lists say why instead of Odoo's "Create a new document": "My evaluations" (no interview to score), "Claims" and "Group mobilities" (nothing open, remove the filter to see the rest) and "Pending validation" (no proposal to validate).
- Help where a field was blank with no hint: placeholders in the call's "Information for applicants" and in the group mobility's activity programme; a line over the project's Documentation table saying what goes there, with the participants' records as one progress bar ("Complete records") instead of three loose numbers; a host entity's internal notes say every teacher reads them (as a student's do).
- Quality panel: the technical paragraph above the tabs is gone; each indicator explains itself on hover (the PDF keeps the full text). In a group mobility's follow-up, each participant shows only their name, not the mobility's name again.
- Teacher's staff calls: no "Accepts applications" column or grey checkbox; the call form shows the "Information for applicants" under its own title, with the rules to download. The upload dialogs no longer say "Files" twice (label and button).
- Student portal: the orange breadcrumb bar on top of every Erasmus+ page now says where you are (Home > Erasmus+ > the call or group mobility) instead of showing only the home icon; the "back to Erasmus+" link it replaces is gone. The commitment letter is folded ("Read the letter"), so the accept box and button stay in sight on a phone.
- A new staff call no longer gets the "Preparation sessions" and "Activities left for your groups" steps: teachers have no preparation sessions, and the activities for their groups are left on Moodle, outside EMS. Calls already created keep theirs.
