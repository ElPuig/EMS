# Changes

## Erasmus+ form headers and smart buttons (issue #784):
- Call: every button shows how many records it opens (applications, ranking, interviews or plan ratings, resolutions, claims, mobilities), and the ranking stays on hand after the definitive resolution.
- Application: the requirements, the total score and the rank are on top; the form's answers and the documents are together in one "Application" tab; a staff application opens the teacher's file from a "Staff record" button instead of repeating their name.
- Mobility: the breadcrumb is just the participant's name and the application opens from a button (the rank stays there); while the destination or the activity is being defined, its empty fields are hidden (the proposal is in its tab; the teacher's own form too). The record is one "N / M" bar, red only when a document is past its deadline, and the student's portal shows the same bar instead of a different count. The EU Survey only shows once the stay is over. The commitment letter says in one line whether it is signed, by whom and when (participant and family), with the signature details below; staff no longer see an empty self-assessment tab.
- Place offers show the time left to answer while pending.
- Group mobility: the dates on one line (planned until the final ones are set, the travel only when it differs); "The centre pays nothing directly" becomes "Who pays the travel and accommodation: the centre / each participant", and the pocket money percentage shows its % sign. The same "signed in one line" for the students' commitment letters.
- Project: the document retention fields moved to the Documentation tab; the Economy tab no longer repeats the economic documents (they are in Documentation) and shows the figures from before EMS only on older projects or when filled in.
- Host entity: "Mobilities" and "Group mobilities" buttons with what it has hosted; the meetings and family relations buttons are hidden on host entities.
