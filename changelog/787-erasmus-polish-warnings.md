# Changes

## Erasmus+ warnings about what is missing (issue #787):
- Teacher's application form dialog: "Save" and "Save and submit" both stay, with "Save and submit" as the highlighted one (P03).
- Mobility (team): a box on top says which step it is at and who has it (destination choice and its turn, proposal waiting for the Direction or the team, documents missing from the participant or the team, documents to review, commitment letter, EU Survey); a warning when it has a destination but no grant amount.
- Teacher's mobility: the same "what to do now" box as their application, and a Steps tab where they tick the steps they have done (and untick their own).
- Group mobility: warnings for participants without a grant, negative pocket money, and before starting the stay (no host entity, start date ahead; never a block); requesting the pocket money asks first whether every collective invoice is in.
- Project economy: a missing grant says so instead of an alarming "above the grant" figure, and missing support rates for the year are one line saying where to add them.
- Application: the exclusion reason on top of the form and in a list column.
- Checked, no change: an offer can no longer get an answer deadline in the past (both ways of creating one already refuse it).
