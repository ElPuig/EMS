# Changes

## Erasmus+ menus in the order of the work (issue #789):
- Calls: Calls, Applications, Ranking, Resolutions, Claims, Place offers, the order of the process (1.1).
- Projects is an entry of its own in the bar, between Mobilities and Financial management, and opens on the economic figures (grant, committed, paid, available, warnings) with the comparison by call; the separate "Economic panel" entry is gone, it was a second list of the same projects (1.4, 10.1).
- Financial management keeps the day-to-day money: Grant payments, Invoices, Expenses (11.1; their new names come with issue #791).
- "Earlier courses" moves from Quality to the end of Configuration as "Earlier course indicators": it is loaded once so the quality panel has a history (1.7, 13.1).
- My participation: "My evaluations" is now "Interviews to score" and "My plan ratings" "Plans to rate" (8.15).
- Quality panel: under the indicators, "Low scores: N, M without an improvement action", which opens those low scores of the panel's selection, the ones without an action first (12.6).
