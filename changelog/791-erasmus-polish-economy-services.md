# Changes

## Erasmus+ services paid by the centre and project expenses (issue #791):
- Named by what they do: "Invoices" is now "Services paid by the centre" (deducted from someone's grant) and "Expenses" "Project expenses" (they touch no grant); they stay separate. On the project they are neighbouring tabs; the expenses left the Economy tab (11.1).
- A service paid by the centre asks one question, "Who it is for: participants / a group mobility", and shows only the field that answers it; the share table only for participants. While nobody is chosen, a warning suggests registering it as a project expense (11.1, 11.2).
- History (chatter) on the grant payment and service forms, e.g. a transfer undone with its reason (11.7).
- The Secretary can also enter services paid by the centre (create and edit, never delete), and reads the individual and group mobilities to say whom they are for (11.11).
- Developer doc: the "Why not Odoo's accounting" section uses the new names.
