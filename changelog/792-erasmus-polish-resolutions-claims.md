# Changes

## Erasmus+ resolutions and claims (issue #792):
- Resolution: the lines of excluded applicants show the exclusion reason to the Erasmus team (4.4). The published list and its PDF still never show it: the call's rules publish the lists without names, and each applicant reads their own reason in the result email and on the portal.
- Claims and appeals: a Result (Upheld / Partially upheld / Dismissed) chosen when answering, required to resolve, shown in the list with colours, in the answer email and on the portal next to the response (4.13). Claims already resolved keep an empty result.
