# What's new:

## Student walkout requests from the portal (issue #800):
- The delegate or sub-delegate of a group calls a student walkout vote from the portal (new "Student walkouts" card and menu entry, /my/vagues), giving only the walkout day and attaching the students' union call; the group votes at once, with no review by the Head of Studies first. The halves of a split group sharing that delegate vote together. Which groups may request one is set by level and, optionally, course in Settings > Student Walkout Settings, seeded from the law (LODE art. 8): ESO from the 3rd year, BTX, CFGB, CFGM, CFGS and EFPS; PFI not.
- Calendar in school hours (weekends, public holidays and school breaks do not count; 24 school hours from a Friday at 13:00 end on the Monday at 13:00), set in Settings > Student Walkout Settings > Calendar: the group votes for one phase (24 school hours by default) and, if it supports the walkout, each student says whether they join for another; the Head of Studies / Deputy Head of Studies gets the minute PDF (centre language) at least the notice before the walkout (48 school hours by default), so a call is opened at least 96 school hours before it. Each phase starts when its email reaches the group (during its classes, or at the start of the next ones), and students can answer on the portal before that too. The portal form shows the calendar and the first possible walkout day before sending, and a call keeps the settings it was opened with.
- The group vote ends early once the votes still to come can no longer change the result, and the joining then starts at once: the minute only ever arrives earlier than projected. The deadlines cron is woken up exactly at each phase's end.
- The group's tutor watches over the call: they get a copy of every email the group gets (when it reaches the group) and can abort the call, with a reason, while it is voted or joined. The wizard explains what the law allows (LODE art. 8: only a formal defect, never the walkout's cause, an exam that day or low attendance). The chain above the tutor (Department or Seminar Chief, Head or Deputy Head of Studies, Director) and the administrators can abort it too; co-tutors of a split group are all told; a group with no tutor goes to every Head and Deputy Head of Studies. The students and the tutor are told, and the Head of Studies gets a notice of each aborted call in their daily summary.
- Reasons come from one configurable catalog for aborting and for not validating (no official call, invalid document, level not included, another sector, another autonomous community, and "Other reasons" with required details; a duplicate call or one opened by someone who is not the delegate are refused by the portal, so they are not reasons).
- The group vote is anonymous (only the counts are kept) and cannot be changed; it supports the walkout with more yes than no among the votes cast. Joining is by name and can be changed until the phase ends. Each student gets an email when the vote opens, and then either the result with the invitation to join or the "not supported" result; the portal home shows a banner while an answer is pending. Students without portal access are listed in the minute but do not answer.
- The Head of Studies hears nothing until the minute. Once closed, if somebody joins, they get a quiet task "Justify the student walkout" and the call's Actions offer "Validate and justify" (one full-day justification of the walkout day for the students who join, skipping and noting anyone already justified that day) and "Do not validate", with a reason. Every student is told either way; the family of each joining minor (or adult sharing with the family) is only told once the call is validated. "Undo the validation" removes the justification and tells students, families and tutor.
- A minor who joins needs their family's ratification (Decret 102/2010, art. 24.2): the family is asked by email at once and ratifies or refuses on the portal (its own "Student walkouts" page and a home banner) until joining ends, and can change its answer meanwhile. A refusal is told to the student. A minor whose family has not ratified it (refused, did not answer in time, or has no portal access) is not justified; the minute shows each minor's ratification. Families asked to ratify are told if the call is later aborted or not validated.
- Emails to students, and the tutor's copies, are only sent during the group's school hours: outside them they are queued until the group's next classes (weekends, public holidays and school breaks skipped). The minute and family emails go at once.
- New backend screen Student's Attendances > Student walkouts (list and read-only form; Actions: Abort the call, Validate and justify, Do not validate, Undo the validation) and Configuration > Student walkouts > Rejection reasons, read within the tutor scope (the group's tutor and the chain above, Director), by every Head of Studies for a group with no tutor, and by the administrators.

# Changes:

## Justification reason "Labour strike" renamed "Student walkout":
- Its English name and xmlid (attendance_justification_reason_strike becomes attendance_justification_reason_walkout) change so that "strike" only means the disciplinary record (Coexistence > Strikes); the Catalan and Spanish names ("Vaga"/"Huelga") stay. A centre that had already renamed it keeps its own name.

# Internal changes:

## Student walkout models, portal and migration:
- New models ems.student_walkout, ems.student_walkout.line, ems.student_walkout.rejection_reason (seeded noupdate) and the wizard ems.student_walkout.reject.wizard; ems.group._ems_school_send_time(moment) picks the mail.mail scheduled_date and each phase's start, ems.datetime_utils.ems_add_school_hours()/ems_school_hours_between() count school hours, res.company.walkout_phase_hours/walkout_notice_hours hold the calendar (one line per student; the group vote is only a counter on the call, never stored per student; concurrent votes rely on REPEATABLE READ and Odoo's retry), controller controllers/portal_student_walkout.py, report ems.report_student_walkout (added to the centre-language reports), nine mail templates (one tutor copy whose text depends on the event), one activity type and the cron _cron_ems_walkout_deadlines (ends the group vote, closes; triggered at each phase's end, hourly as a safety net).
- ems.level gets walkout_allowed / walkout_course_from / walkout_course_to (ems.group._ems_walkout_allowed()).
- Manifest 18.0.0.49.0: migrations/18.0.0.49.0/pre-migrate.py renames the reason's xmlid (and its English name while still the seeded one); post-migrate.py seeds the walkout levels, the same as post_init_hook on a new installation.
- Tests: TestStudentWalkout (35), TestPortalStudentWalkout (7), TestStudentWalkoutTour (the tutor aborts with a reason, the Head of Studies validates), ACCESS_MATRIX rows (the reject wizard open to teachers, who may act checked in Python; the rejection reasons readable by the system administrator); the justification tour follows the renamed reason. Developer doc: docs/en/developers/attendance/student_walkout.md.
- ems.base.get_user_is_top_admin(): the system or the academic administrator, at the top of the permission pyramid; the walkout uses it (the same condition is still written by hand in several other models).
- Screenshots of the three manuals regenerated by TestDocsScreenshotsStudentWalkout (tagged -standard, run by hand).
