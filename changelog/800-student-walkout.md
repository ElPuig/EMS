# What's new:

## Student walkout requests from the portal (issue #800):
- The delegate or sub-delegate of a group requests a student walkout from the portal (new "Student walkouts" card and menu entry, /my/vagues), giving only the walkout day and attaching the students' union call, at least 72 hours before the walkout. The halves of a split group sharing that delegate vote together. Which groups may request one is set by level and, optionally, course in Settings > Student Walkout Settings, seeded from the law (LODE art. 8): ESO from the 3rd year, BTX, CFGB, CFGM, CFGS and EFPS; PFI not.
- Fixed calendar, one day per phase, each starting as soon as the previous one ends: the Head of Studies / Deputy Head of Studies accepts or rejects the request (by 3 days before the walkout, or it expires), the group votes whether it supports the walkout (by 2 days before; it ends early once everybody has voted), and, if it does, each student says whether they join (by the day before). Then the minute PDF (centre language) goes to the Head of Studies.
- Nobody in the group hears of a request until the Head of Studies accepts it (email at once and a quiet task to review it). A rejection needs a reason from a configurable catalog (no official call, invalid document, level not included, another sector, another autonomous community, duplicate, and "Other reasons" with required details). Before the vote only the delegate is told, so a misuse of the tool reaches nobody else; the Head of Studies can still reject it at any later moment, even once justified, and then the group (and, once closed, the families) are told and a justification already generated is removed.
- The group vote is anonymous (only the counts are kept) and cannot be changed; it supports the walkout with more yes than no among the votes cast. Joining is by name and can be changed until the phase ends. Each student gets an email when the vote opens, and then either the result with the invitation to join or the "not supported" result; the portal home shows a banner while an answer is pending. Students without portal access are listed in the minute but do not answer.
- Once closed, if somebody joins, the Head of Studies gets a quiet task "Justify the student walkout" and the call's Actions offer "Generate the justification": one full-day justification of the walkout day for the students who join, skipping (and noting) anyone already justified that day. The family of each joining minor (or adult sharing with the family) is told.
- Emails to students are only sent during their group's school hours: outside them they are queued until the group's next classes (weekends, public holidays and school breaks skipped). Staff and family emails go at once.
- New backend screen Student's Attendances > Student walkouts (list and read-only form; Actions: Accept, Reject, Generate the justification) and Configuration > Student walkouts > Rejection reasons, read within the tutor scope (the group's tutor and the chain above, Director) and by the administrators; only the Head of Studies / Deputy / Director in that scope, or an administrator, accepts, rejects and justifies.

# Changes:

## Justification reason "Labour strike" renamed "Student walkout":
- Its English name and xmlid (attendance_justification_reason_strike becomes attendance_justification_reason_walkout) change so that "strike" only means the disciplinary record (Coexistence > Strikes); the Catalan and Spanish names ("Vaga"/"Huelga") stay. A centre that had already renamed it keeps its own name.

# Internal changes:

## Student walkout models, portal and migration:
- New models ems.student_walkout, ems.student_walkout.line, ems.student_walkout.rejection_reason (seeded noupdate) and the wizard ems.student_walkout.reject.wizard; ems.group._ems_school_send_time() picks the mail.mail scheduled_date (one line per student; the group vote is only a counter on the call, never stored per student; concurrent votes rely on REPEATABLE READ and Odoo's retry), controller controllers/portal_student_walkout.py, report ems.report_student_walkout (added to the centre-language reports), eight mail templates, two activity types and the hourly cron _cron_ems_walkout_deadlines (expires, ends the group vote, closes).
- ems.level gets walkout_allowed / walkout_course_from / walkout_course_to (ems.group._ems_walkout_allowed()).
- Manifest 18.0.0.48.0: migrations/18.0.0.48.0/pre-migrate.py renames the reason's xmlid (and its English name while still the seeded one); post-migrate.py seeds the walkout levels, the same as post_init_hook on a new installation.
- Tests: TestStudentWalkout (24), TestPortalStudentWalkout (6), the Head of Studies tour TestStudentWalkoutTour (accept, reject with a reason, justify), ACCESS_MATRIX rows; the justification tour follows the renamed reason. Developer doc: docs/en/developers/attendance/student_walkout.md.
