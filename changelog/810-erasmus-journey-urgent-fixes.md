# Changes

## Erasmus+ staff screens in use during the 2026-27 calls (journey review):

- The staff application and activity-proposal dialogs show every field's line and an example of what to write in each one; the upload dialog says it takes a PDF or a photo, and several files.
- The "Hand in", "Sign it here" and "PDF" buttons fit in their lists without scrolling the table.
- A teacher's application shows only what they handed in and what is left to do; the team's review columns stay in the team's form. The missing state reads "To hand in" everywhere, the portal included.
- The plan rating form shows each proposal under its title at full width.
- The staff ranking uses short criterion headers, with the full name on hover.
- The staff resolution has no empty modality column and its score header reads "Score".
- The family's portal and the coordinator's "From the application" block also say "To hand in" instead of "Missing".
- The activity in the proposal dialog has an example too, greyed like the other examples.
- The quality panel's filters show their field lines; the satisfaction and low-score lists, and "Earlier courses", use short headers with the full name on hover; the staff columns of "Earlier courses" are hidden at first and can be shown from the columns menu.
- A teacher's mobility record says in words how many of their own documents they have handed in ("You have handed in 0 of 4"): what the Erasmus team uploads or prepares, and the application's documents, no longer count. The team's columns only show once there is something in them.
- A teacher sees their grant in their mobility, read only: what they are due, what has been paid and what is left; never the invoices or the payments themselves.
- The project's economy warning reads "N requested payments still to make", and its figure "Committed, not paid yet" explains the difference in its help.
- The invoices list shows the project of the invoice's participants, or "Various" when they are of several.
