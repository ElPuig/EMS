# Changes

## Erasmus+ staff screens in use during the 2026-27 calls (journey review):

- The staff application and activity-proposal dialogs show every field's line and an example of what to write in each one; the upload dialog says it takes a PDF or a photo, and several files.
- The "Hand in", "Sign it here" and "PDF" buttons fit in their lists without scrolling the table.
- A teacher's application shows only what they handed in and what is left to do; the team's review columns stay in the team's form. The missing state reads "To hand in" everywhere, the portal included.
- The plan rating form shows each proposal under its title at full width.
- The staff ranking uses short criterion headers, with the full name on hover.
- The staff resolution has no empty modality column and its score header reads "Score".
