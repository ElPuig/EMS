# What's new

## Justified student who comes to class (marked as attending, with undo):
- In the roll-call, a justified absence can now be switched between Attended, Minor Delay, Severe Delay and Justified Miss when the student comes to class anyway; only the ordinary Miss stays locked. A warning asks for confirmation when leaving Justified Miss, the student's tutor is told in their daily summary, and choosing Justified Miss again restores the absence as if nothing had happened.
- The line keeps its justification, so removing the justification later leaves the attendance as it is. The session's teacher, a guard teacher, the student's tutor and the chiefs above them, and the administrator can do it.
