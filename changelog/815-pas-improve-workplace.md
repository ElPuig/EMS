# Changes

## Work location linked to the department (issue #815):

- Departments now have a Work Location; every member takes it as their own work location (read-only on the employee). A department without one leaves its members' current value untouched.
- The field is hidden on the PAS form, where it only repeated the department.
- The "Work locations" menu entry and the new field are translated into Catalan and Spanish.
