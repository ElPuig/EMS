# Fixes

## Tutor loses the closed ex officio case file after the withdrawal:
- The case file now stores its tutor (it follows the group's tutor while the student has a group and keeps the last one when they leave), and the tutor-scope rules of the case file, its steps, reviews and checks use it. The tutor, the Department/Seminar Chief and Head of Studies above them and Direction keep opening the closed case file and its history during the course of the withdrawal; another tutor never does.
- A migration restores the tutor of case files already closed from the student's academic history.
- A Head of Studies or Deputy Head of Studies outside the tutor's chain still reads every case file, but can no longer decide the proposal, review the roll-calls or do the tutor's and Coexistence's steps in it.
- Tutors' manual (ca/es/en) says where to find a closed case file (remove the "Open" filter) and until when it stays visible.
