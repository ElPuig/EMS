# What's new

## Warning to the Department and Seminar Chiefs before an absence starts:

- The Department Chief and the Seminar Chief of the employee's department receive an email shortly before a colleague's absence starts, so they can arrange the cover. It is sent once per absence, and again if its dates change. How long before it starts is a setting (Settings, Staff Absence Settings, 2 hours by default, 0 switches it off).
