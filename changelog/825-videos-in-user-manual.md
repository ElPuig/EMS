# Internal changes

## Closing table is a gate, not a report:
- The project instructions now say that a ⚠️ or ❌ row of the closing table is work (do it in full or ask the developer), that a ✅ needs its evidence and cannot carry a caveat, and that a conditional go-ahead ("if the CI is green, deploy") does not waive the table. Added after v18.0.0.49.0 was merged with rows still in ⚠️.
