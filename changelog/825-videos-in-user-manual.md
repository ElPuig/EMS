# Internal changes

## Closing table is a gate, not a report:
- The project instructions now say that a ⚠️ or ❌ row of the closing table is work (do it in full or ask the developer), that a ✅ needs its evidence and cannot carry a caveat, and that a conditional go-ahead ("if the CI is green, deploy") does not waive the table. Added after v18.0.0.49.0 was merged with rows still in ⚠️.

# Changes

## Videos in the user manuals:
- The manuals now link the existing videos (guard duties, ex officio withdrawal, student walkout) from the page of the role they are for, in Catalan, Spanish and English. Each list of videos is preceded by a warning that they may be out of date, since EMS is updated more often than the videos are remade.
