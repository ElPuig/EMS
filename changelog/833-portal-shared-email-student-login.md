# Fixes

## Portal for students sharing their email with a family member (#833):

- A student whose main email is also a family member's (frequent at enrolment, when the centre gets one address for the whole family) could not get into the portal at all: the family member was granted first and took that email as login, the student's grant then failed with Odoo's generic "same email as an existing user" (only listed among the wizard's issues), and without a portal user Google sign-in had nothing to link to either.
- New rule: the login with the shared email is always the family's; the student's portal user gets the corporate email (`student_email`) as login, so the student signs in with "Log in with Google" or with the corporate email and the portal password. A student with their own personal email keeps it as login, unchanged.
- A student with no corporate email yet gets no user, with a clear message in the wizard's notification and preview; their access is granted automatically when the Google account is created (only when the family already has portal access, and never to a student whose access was revoked).
- Every path that grants or realigns a portal login follows the rule: the portal access wizard, a changed main email, undoing a withdrawal, the contact data request, Odoo's own "Grant portal access" action (`portal.wizard.user` inherited) and a changed corporate email. Granting the family takes the shared login back from a student who held it. Each recipient now runs inside a savepoint, so a failed one leaves no half-done writes.
- A yellow warning at the top of the student form when the main email is a family member's (a warning only, never a block), and a note on the wizard's preview line.
- The Google credentials emails keep going to the shared address (covered by a test). End-to-end HTTP tests sign in by password and through Google: a student (sharing the email or not) always lands on their own portal, walkouts included, and the family member with the shared email on the family portal (ratifying, never proposing).
- Migration 18.0.0.49.3 fixes existing installations: students holding the shared login move to the corporate one and the family members they kept out are granted; students left without a user get one with the corporate login. On the dev database: 443 students/ex-students share the email, 94 accesses granted, 84 students with the corporate email as login. Odoo's own "login changed" security email reaches the shared address for each moved login, telling the family the student's new login.
- Developer docs (`portal_access_wizard.md`, `google_workspace_student.md`) and the tutors' portal access manual and secretary's contacts manual (ca/es/en) updated.
