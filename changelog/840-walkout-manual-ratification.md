# What's new:
## Manual ratification of a minor's walkout by the tutor (families that cannot use the portal):
- The tutor, whoever is above them in the hierarchy and the administrators can mark by hand the ratification of a minor who joins a student walkout, with a button on the Students tab of the proposal, for families that cannot access the portal and ask the centre.
- It is possible from when students join until the Head of Studies validates the walkout, only while the family has not answered (a refusal is never overridden). The row shows that the centre ratified it and who did, and the change is logged in the proposal's chatter. If the family answers on the portal later, or the student withdraws, the manual mark is cleared.
