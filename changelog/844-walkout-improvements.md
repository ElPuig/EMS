# Changes:

## Student walkout: off the roll-call instead of justified (issue #844):
- Every student who joins a walkout is off the roll-calls of its day ("Not required to attend: student walkout", the #644 archived-line mechanism) from the moment joining closes with the group's support: neither an absence nor a presence. Roll-calls taken later start that way (`res.partner._ems_not_expected_reasons` override, `models/contacts/student_walkout_attendance.py`); lines already taken are updated on closing, and a joining extended after closing syncs them again.
- Nothing is justified any more: the Head of Studies' "Validate and justify" is now "Validate" (`action_validate`, state `justified` renamed `validated`, task "Validate the student walkout"). Not validating it, or undoing the validation, turns those classes into regular absences (without emailing families about past days).
- The family's ratification of a minor is now informative only (it says the family is aware and agrees; nobody can revoke the decision). What it changes: the family of a minor who joins without it (pending, refused, no portal) gets the usual attendance notice of each class of that day, with a dedicated, purely informative text ("collective decision not to attend class", "it is not an absence nor a sanction"); the roll-call label tells the teacher. A ratification marked by hand later withdraws a pending notice.
- Removing a student from the roll-call again after putting them back by mistake recovers the reason that still applies (walkout, expected withdrawal or convalidation) and its notice; the roll-call reloads the line to show it.
- In Catalan and Spanish the feature no longer calls the students' decision a "vaga"/"huelga", for students, families and staff alike (portal, emails, the minute, menus, forms, settings, roll-call label, manuals): it is a "decisió col·lectiva d'inassistència a classe" (short: "inassistència col·lectiva"); only what a union calls stays a "convocatòria de vaga". The staff strike report and the "Vaga" justification reason are real strikes and keep the word. The family's emails quote the reason instead of assuming a union's call.
- Texts for students and families (portal, emails, manuals) rewritten: a collective decision not to attend class that nobody can revoke, the classes of that day do not count as absences, and, for families, once ratified there is nothing else to do.

## Student walkout: any supporting document, the reason in every banner (issue #844):
- What the group votes is a collective decision not to attend class that day (LODE, art. 8), not support for a union's strike: a union's call is one possible reason among others. The proposal still requires a reason and a document, but the document may be any that supports the decision (a students' union's call, the minute of the group's assembly...).
- The portal banners (vote pending, joining open, family asked to ratify) and the students' vote email quote the reason in full; the vote question, the proposal form, the page, the minute and the emails say that the group decides whether it walks out.
- Rejection reasons: "No official call of a students' union for that day" is archived and "The attached document is not a valid call" becomes "The attached document does not support the proposal" (a migration renames it unless the centre already had). The reasons that judged who calls it or where ("the call does not include the group's level or studies", "for another sector", "for another autonomous community") are archived too by the same migration: only the procedure is judged, never the cause, who calls it or the territory. The abort wizard, the tutor's email and the manuals say so.

## Student walkout: the family's answer is an acknowledgement of receipt (issue #844):
- The family of a minor who joins is no longer asked to "ratify" it (a word that sounds like an authorisation the exercise of the right does not need): it acknowledges receipt on the portal and says "I agree" or "I do not agree". Portal, emails, the form's button ("Family agrees (by hand)"), the report, the roll-call label, the daily summary, the manuals and the translations say so; the technical keys keep their names. The behavior does not change: the answer never revokes the decision, and the family that has not said it agrees is told of each class, for information.
- A student whose family first did not agree and then does is told by email that no absence will be notified (the portal notice already changed with the answer).
- The abort wizard no longer cites the Decret 102/2010 and says that a proposal is never aborted because of who calls it or where.
- The same group-vote route stays: each group's delegate starts the vote (the NOFC will say so; a vote of all the delegates is impossible with morning and afternoon shifts).

## Student walkout portal: answers confirmed in a pop-up (issue #844):
- After voting, saying whether one joins, extending the joining or ratifying, the page pops up what was saved and what comes next (`saved=<vote|join|extend|ratified|refused>`), and the joining form and the family's buttons fold under the answer with a "Change my answer" button while joining lasts.

# Internal changes:

## Student walkout migration (issue #844):
- `migrations/18.0.0.50.0/pre-migrate.py` renames the state `justified` to `validated` (also as `rejected_from_state`) and the task's xmlid; `post-migrate.py` deletes the justifications validated walkouts had created, drops `ems_student_walkout.justification_id` and takes the classes of the students who joined closed or validated walkouts off the roll-call, without notifying families.
