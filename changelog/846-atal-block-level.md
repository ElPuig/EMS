# Changes

## Schedule declaration: level per student-support (ATAL) block:

- A tutoring block of a teacher's schedule (Tutorship Time, Student support) can now name the level it is given to (ESO, Bachillerato...), from its card on the Schedule tab or from the attendance form.
- The official schedule declaration counts those hours in that level's tutoring row, so a teacher with student support in two levels is declared correctly. A block without a level keeps the previous rule (tutored group's level, or the level with most class hours).
- Re-importing a schedule file clears the levels set by hand on its non-teaching blocks.
