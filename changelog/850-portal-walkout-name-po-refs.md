# Fixes

## Portal still showed the old name of student walkouts (missing view references in the .po files):
- The "Student walkouts" block of `i18n/ca_ES.po`/`es_ES.po` had no reference to the four portal views that use it (`ems_portal_custom_header_menu`, `portal_my_home_ems_custom_landing`, `portal_student_walkouts`, `portal_student_walkout_family`). Odoo binds a view term to its exact `#:` reference, so those views kept the old translation "Vagues d'alumnes" / "Huelgas de estudiantes" in the portal menu, home card, list title and family screen. Same for "Walkout day" in the proposal form (`portal_student_walkout_new`) and the PDF (`report_student_walkout_document`), still "Dia de la vaga" / "Día de la huelga". Both blocks now list every view, and their duplicated references were removed.
- Six Catalan/Spanish texts about the group's proposal still said "proposta de suport a una convocatòria de vaga" / "propuesta de apoyo a una convocatoria de huelga" (no proposals in the group, a proposal already existing for that day, the group vote closed, the cancel confirmation, who may propose, "say whether the group supports it"). They now use "proposta d'inassistència col·lectiva" / "propuesta de inasistencia colectiva", like the rest of the feature; "vaga" stays only for real strikes (staff strike report, union call).
- No migration needed: every deploy runs `upgrade.sh`, which reloads the module's translations and overwrites the stored ones. Verified with a DB read-back of the seven views after `./upgrade.sh`.
