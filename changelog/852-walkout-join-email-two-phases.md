# Changes

## Student walkout joining email explains the two phases (issue #852):

- Some students took the group vote in favour for their own joining. The email sent when joining opens now says that the group vote is only the first step, that each student now answers personally on the portal before the deadline (changeable until then), and what each answer means, as a short bold list: joining, the day's classes do not count as absences and the name appears in the minute; not joining, or not answering, the student must go to class and absences count as usual. For minors, the family's acknowledgement is for information only and does not change the student's answer. New subject: "now you have to say whether you join it".
- The same clarity in the related texts: the vote email and the portal's vote card and confirmation say that voting yes is not joining; the "joining extended" email tells students who already answered that they need do nothing, and the others that not answering is not joining; the portal's join card lists the consequences of each answer; the home banner says that not answering is not joining; the result card says "You did not answer, so you do not join it" for a student who never answered.
- The group vote is "voted in favour" everywhere students read it (portal, emails, the minute's result line), never "decided", which sounded like something already done.
- Families' (students' and delegates') and tutors' manuals updated in Catalan, Spanish and English, with the vote screenshot regenerated; a broken link to the families' manual in the Catalan Head of Studies' manual fixed.
- A test confirms that a student with portal access who never answers does not join: not in the minute and still on the roll-call of the day.
