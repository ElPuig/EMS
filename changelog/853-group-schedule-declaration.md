# What's new

## Official schedule declaration of groups (pages 5 and 6 of the Departament's "Annexos Dades anuals"):

- Heads and Deputy Heads of Studies, the Director and the administrators can print the group's declaration, ready to sign and always in Catalan: ESO, Batxillerat and PFI groups get "Horari grup d'alumnes" (with the level, acronym, course and group letter, laid out like the cycles' page) and the vocational cycles get "Distribució horària dels Cicles Formatius" (degree, study, number of students, course hours and each teacher's weekly hours when several share a module).
- Print it from Educational Community → Groups (select several main groups, Print → Schedule declaration, one page each) or from a group's Actions dropdown. Only main groups have one; their splits and optatives already appear in their schedule. The cycles' page takes the course's duration from the study's new official **Duration (hours)** (2000 h for the cycles, 600 h for the videogame specialisation course, from the Departament's curriculum). Classes given in parallel are grouped under one merged slot cell, in this declaration and in the group and student schedule PDFs.
- The administrators can now read the groups (they could print the declaration but not open the list).

# Internal changes

## Shared header, footer and print permission of the schedule declarations:

- The Departament's header and legal footer of the teacher's declaration are now shared templates used by the groups' one, and the "who can print" check lives in one place for both.
