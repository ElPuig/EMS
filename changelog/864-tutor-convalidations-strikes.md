# What's new

## Tutors read the convalidation requests of their students:
- A tutor now reads the convalidation requests of the students they tutor, from the Convalidations button of the student's form and from Academic management > Convalidations. They see the request as the student sees it on the portal: the original request, the step it has reached and, only once the resolution is official, the outcome per subject.
- What is decided before it is official stays hidden on the server, not just in the views: the subjects, grades, remarks, resolution documents, proposal stamps and the chatter are readable only by the Heads of Studies, the Director, the secretariat and the administrator, so a tutor cannot read them by any other route either.
- The portal and the tutor's summary are rendered from one shared template, so the two never drift apart.
- The model had no record rules before; the tutor's is limited to their tutees (the tutor scope of the whole application) and the Heads of Studies, Director, secretariat and administrator get explicit unrestricted ones.

## Tutors delete the strikes of their students:
- A tutor can delete any strike given to one of their students, whoever issued it, and the ones they issued themselves. A teacher who is not a tutor still cannot.

# Changes

## Convalidations button always shown on the student form:
- The button now also appears with a zero when the student has no request, like the strikes one, and is shown to tutors too. Its count follows the user's own permissions, so it never shows a number that the list behind it would not open.
