# What's new

## Grade claims from the portal (teacher, then tutor):

- During an evaluation's claims period, a student (or the family of a minor, following the convalidations' rule) can claim from the portal the grade of a learning outcome evaluated in that round, and the module's final grade once every outcome is evaluated: one claim per grade and evaluation.
- The portal makes clear that a claim is not asking for a higher grade but pointing out that the grade does not match the calculation the teacher shared, and asks to explain what does not match.
- The session's teacher answers first, from Grades > Grade claims: accept with the corrected grade (written in the published session; for the final grade, as the overridden centre grade) or deny with a reason. After a denial, the student can take the claim to the tutor until the end of the claims period, or two school days after the denial if that is later. The Head of Studies of the tutor's area can answer either instance.
- Denial reasons are a catalog the Head of Studies edits and reorders (Evaluation management > Claim denial reasons); "Others" asks for details.
- Teachers and tutors get the claim as a quiet task in their daily summary; the student (and the family of a minor) gets an email with each answer.
- An evaluation cannot be finalised while any claim is unanswered.
- The publication announcement email now tells how to claim a grade.
