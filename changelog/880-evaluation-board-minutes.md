# What's new

## Evaluation board minutes:

- New minute type "Acta de junta d'avaluació", replacing the evaluation tabs of the tutor's spreadsheet: when the Head of Studies moves a group's sessions to the board, EMS creates that board's minute for the group's tutor (once per group and evaluation), with a quiet task in their daily summary until it is sent for approval.
- The minute comes filled in: the teaching team's attendance, the four difficulty areas (A-D) to describe, the term's statistics (withdrawals with their reasons, students missing more than 25% of their hours, students with unjustified absences above 5%), and every student with the outcomes passed and evaluated in the round, which can be refreshed from the grades during the board.
- Per student, the tutor marks whether they work (counted in the statistics) and writes the teaching team's comments, which stay in the minute, and a comment for the student and the family, shown in the portal and the grades' PDF under that evaluation's grades once they are published.
- The minute's PDF prints the difficulties, the statistics and the teaching team's comments.
