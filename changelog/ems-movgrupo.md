# What's new

## Erasmus+ group mobilities of students, KA121-VET (#701, #702, #703, #704):

- New *Erasmus+ > Group mobilities* (`ems.erasmus.group.mobility`): a third Erasmus+ modality next to the internship and staff calls. The coordinator creates each mobility with its KA121-VET project, the real EMS groups whose students can enrol (`group_ids`, e.g. the two morning SMX2 groups; the shift only filters the choice), student places (8) and accompanying teachers (2), the leader teacher and the other accompanying teacher, the enrolment period, the published selection criteria, destination, host entity, planned and final dates, travel dates and the activity programme. Destination and dates can stay pending. The four initial mobilities (SMX2 and 2GA, morning and afternoon) are configuration, not code.
- States: draft, enrolment, selection, waiting for validation, preparation, during the stay, on return, closed (or cancelled).
- One-click enrolment from the portal (`/my/erasmus`): each student sees only the group mobilities of their groups, enrols with "Inscriure'm" (optional, confidential fewer-opportunities declaration) and can withdraw while the period is open; the record stays. Server checks: logged-in student, eligible group, period open, no duplicate, the internship call's conduct rule (as many conduct warnings this course as the coexistence threshold, or more). No CV, letter, video nor interview.
- Ranking when the enrolment closes (hourly cron or the coordinator's button): EMS freezes each enrolment's group, record average (the internship call's own formula, extracted as `_ems_partner_grade_values`/`_ems_grade_average` without changing it) and this course's conduct warnings; non-eligible students are excluded with the reason (only the Erasmus team sees it). Order: average, then fewer opportunities accredited by the team, then a tie order the coordination sets: a remaining tie or an unchecked fewer-opportunities declaration blocks the proposal, EMS never breaks it silently. The first ones are proposed as selected, the rest as reserves; "excluded" and "no place" are different results.
- Selection: the leader teacher reviews the ranking (rank, average and eligibility; never conduct data) and sends the proposal; only the Erasmus coordinator approves it, sends it back with a reason or sets a justified exception. Approving publishes each student's own result by email and on the portal. Withdrawals free the place; EMS shows the next reserve, the leader proposes them and the coordinator approves; the frozen ranking is never rewritten and every step keeps who and when.
- Participants' files (`ems.erasmus.group.participant`) for each selected student and both teachers, who join without any staff call. Their documents are lines of the existing Erasmus+ document engine, now possible without an application: a line belongs to an application or to a group mobility (SQL check), a participant's own or the group's shared one. The group learning agreement exists once for the whole group; default documents per role (students: grant agreement, health card, family authorisations for minors, insurance, Europass mobility document; teachers: grant agreement, certificate of stay), editable per mobility. Students upload theirs from the portal and download what the team prepares.
- Follow-up during the stay (`ems.erasmus.group.log`: attendance, incidents apart from the conduct warnings, changes, evidence, communications), EU Survey task for the leader (deadline, answered, validated by the team, evidence), proposed closure by the leader and closure by the team, collective expenses as invoices linked to the group mobility and never split.
- Coordinator overview: kanban by state and list with groups, leader, enrolled and selected students, documents missing and to review, ties, EU Survey and late tasks.
- Permissions: a new hidden group (`ems.group_erasmus_group_teacher`) managed by EMS gives the leader and accompanying teachers *Erasmus+ > My group mobilities*; record rules keep each teacher to their own mobilities and away from confidential documents; only the leader sees the enrolments; what each role may change is checked again in the models. The Erasmus team can now read the academic groups (`ems.group`) to choose them.
- Manuals for the Erasmus team, the group's teachers and the students, in Catalan, Spanish and English; developer doc `docs/en/developers/erasmus/group_mobility.md` with the integration contract for the document archive work.
