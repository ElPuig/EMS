# Changes

## Erasmus+ staff call: job shadowings and teaching score more than courses, no internationalisation criterion:

- The panel's rubric for the mobility plan (criterion 1, annex 2) has a third aspect, "Activity of proposal A": 1 point when proposal A is a job shadowing or a teaching assignment, 0 when it is a course. Only proposal A counts, and nothing checks it later against the activity chosen on the mobility. The plan is now out of 3.
- Criterion 2 (last staff mobility: never or more than two courses ago) goes from 1.5 to 1 point at most.
- Criterion 6 (internationalisation) is gone: its merit kind, its column in the ranking, the resolutions and the portal. Its point is split between criterion 3 (centre projects, still up to 1.5) and criterion 4 (languages: B2 1.5, C1 2, C2 2.5). The staff criteria still add up to 10.
- The application form says that a job shadowing or a teaching assignment in proposal A scores one more point, and its examples now put the job shadowing in A and the course in B.
- Migration: deletes the internationalisation merits, recomputes the stored scores of existing staff applications (published resolution lines keep their frozen copy) and resets the old "Score (max 2)" translations so the .po can load the new ones.
- Admin, plan-rating and applicant manuals (ca/es/en), the developer doc and the rubric screenshot updated.
