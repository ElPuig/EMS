# Changes

## Erasmus+ group mobilities on the document records (integration of ems-carpetas and ems-movgrupo):

- Group mobilities use the records' structure: their documents get the four participant categories (`file_category`, replacing the removed `sepie_folder` in the default requirements: health card, inclusion and minors' authorisation in 01, group travel and common evidence in 03, the rest by stage), the same `file_state`, the same history rules (files kept on a valid line or a closed group mobility, every review written in the group mobility's chatter with the participant) and the same upload checks. The group's own "missing" logic was replaced by `file_state`, so a shared document the team uploads is complete as in the individual records.
- The health card of a group student is confidential, as in the calls: the leader and the accompanying teacher no longer see it.
- A document someone has to sign is never complete only because a file was uploaded, whoever uploaded it: the team's own documents with signers (group learning agreement, Europass, sBID REF5/REF6) now wait for the team to mark them valid, in the individual mobilities too. The documents the team provides that nobody signs (insurance policy, final list) are still complete once uploaded.
- The project ZIP includes every group mobility that is neither draft nor cancelled (`02_Mobilitats/GRUP<id>_<name>/`): the common documents once in `00_Documentacio_comuna` (shared documents, `Selection.csv` and `Draws.csv` with the frozen ranking, the follow-up with its files, the EU Survey evidence) and each active participant's record, which references the shared documents instead of copying them. A group mobility can also be exported alone (*Actions > Export the documents*). Conduct, fewer-opportunities and exclusion data, the health card and the inclusion documents only appear in an audit export. The export log, the missing count and the project's record counters include the group mobilities.
- Commitment letter versions: when a group mobility's destination, host, dates, travel dates, teachers or additional conditions change after a letter was accepted, the acceptance is kept unchanged as an earlier version (`ems.erasmus.commitment.version`: exact text, who, when, IP, family included, why it was replaced; it cannot be changed nor deleted) and the student and the family accept the new letter (the family is emailed). An acceptance while the destination or the dates are still to be confirmed is provisional and never lets the student leave. The ZIP's letter page includes every earlier version. Internships are unchanged.
- Development databases that still had the old SEPIE folder of each call document get the category from it on upgrade (`_auto_init`, before Odoo drops the column); never needed in production, where Erasmus+ is not released yet.
