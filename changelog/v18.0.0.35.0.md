# Internal changes:

## Time-of-day independent tests (shift_ems_clock):
- Roll-call, strike, attendance issue/notification/report, justification, year record and
  presence tests depended on the real clock: their whole-day slot ended at 23:00 and their
  weekday came from date.today() (UTC), so they failed after 23:00 and between local midnight
  and 02:00, blocking a night release.
- New tests/common.py helper shift_ems_clock() moves every clock a test reads (EMS's own, the
  browser's serverNow() and odoo.fields' now/today, field defaults included) to 10:00 of the
  company's day, always forward, and lets it run; fixtures take their date and weekday from it.
- The roll-call tours now wait for the slot to be offered before picking it (a race hidden by
  the real clock). Verified with faketime at 00:30, 09:30 and 23:30 local.
