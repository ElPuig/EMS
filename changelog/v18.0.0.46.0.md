# Fixes

## Convalidation requests from the portal went to the Erasmus+ page:

- The portal's convalidation and Erasmus+ controllers both set a `_redirect` attribute, and Odoo merges every `CustomerPortal` subclass into one class, so the Erasmus+ value won: submitting or answering a convalidation request ended on `/my/erasmus`. Each controller has its own attribute now.

# Internal changes

## Integration of v18.0.0.46.0:

- `res.partner._ems_informed_recipients()` is the one name for the student-and-family recipients (Erasmus+ and #726 had renamed `_ems_convalidation_recipients()` differently); the minute's draft email (#755) uses the centre-language helper of #749.
- Erasmus+ payments show the IBAN grouped by four whether or not `base_iban` is installed; the Secretary's payment tasks use `ems.base.role_task_users()`.
- Tests: a staff call test no longer depends on the database having a Director; guidance and coexistence keep Academic management for the ex officio withdrawals; the tutor tour opens Authorizations explicitly (Academic management now opens on Ex officio withdrawals for a tutor); the guard duty board tour waits for the router to push the URL back after leaving a notice.
- Catalan and Spanish wording found by LanguageTool in the integrated texts.
- Erasmus+ staff tasks are scheduled quietly (no "assigned to you" email, 10 places) and the tutor's notice when a student accepts a place and the evaluators' list of interviews go to the daily summary (`ems.info_notice`) instead of an email on the spot: nothing in Erasmus+ is urgent for the staff (developer, 2026-11-05).
- Integrating #762 and #764: the pocket money task for the Secretary is scheduled quietly too, the account number grouping is one helper (`payee.group_iban`) for individual and group payments, the Erasmus+ portal page keeps `_erasmus_redirect`, and two Catalan wordings found by LanguageTool.
