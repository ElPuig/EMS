# Attendance Correction Requests

Sometimes a check-in or check-out gets recorded with the wrong time (a forgotten clock-in, a kiosk glitch, etc.). Teachers can request a correction directly from their own attendance record; a Head of Studies (or Deputy Head of Studies) then accepts or rejects the request.

**Required role:** Teacher (to request); Head of Studies, Deputy Head of Studies, Director or Administrator (to decide)

---

## Requesting a Correction

1. Navigate to **Employee Attendances** and open one of your own attendance records.
2. Click **Request Correction** in the header.
3. Fill in the corrected **Check-in** and/or **Check-out** time (only fill in the ones that are wrong) and explain the **Reason**. Only the *time of day* can be corrected — the date always stays the one from the original record.
4. Click **Save**. The request is now **Pending** and has been sent to the person who can validate it. The reason can no longer be edited once saved.

> You will see your past requests and their status under **Employee Attendances → Correction Requests**, and on the attendance record itself via the **Corrections** button in the header.

---

## Deciding on a Request (Head of Studies / Deputy / Director / Administrator)

1. Navigate to **Employee Attendances → Correction Requests** (or the **Corrections** button on the attendance record itself). Pending requests assigned to you also appear as an **Activity** to-do.
2. Open a request to review the original vs. requested times and the reason given.
3. Click **Accept** to apply the correction to the attendance record, or **Reject** to leave it unchanged (or restore it, if you're reversing a previous acceptance). You can optionally leave a note for the teacher.
4. The teacher who made the request is notified of the decision automatically.

> Made a mistake? **Accept** and **Reject** stay available after a decision, so you can change your mind at any time — clicking the other button reverses the previous decision.

> Any Head of Studies, Deputy Head of Studies, Director or Administrator can decide on any pending request — this is not restricted to "your own" teachers in the current version.
