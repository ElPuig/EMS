# Centre Language

The **Centre language** is the language EMS writes in by itself, whatever language each user works in:

- the codes of minutes and agreements,
- the minutes, their PDF and their calls,
- the convalidation resolutions,
- the public schedules of the groups,
- the texts EMS adds on its own (for example, the follow-up of an agreement closed in a meeting).

It is also the language new contacts and new users get. Each person can still choose their own language
in their preferences.

**Required role:** Administrator

---

## Access

Navigate to: **Settings → EMS Management → Center Data → Centre language**

---

## Change the centre language

1. Pick the language in **Centre language**. Only the languages installed in Odoo appear.
2. Save.

The setting can never be empty: it is Catalan unless you choose another one. Changing it does not translate
what was already written: it applies to what EMS writes from then on.
