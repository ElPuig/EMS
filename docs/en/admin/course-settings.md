# Current Course

The **Current course** setting controls which academic year (e.g. `2025-2026`) is used for day-to-day operations: attendance, grades, and incident tracking.

**Required role:** Administrator

---

## Access

Navigate to: **Settings → EMS Management → Course Management Settings**

![Course Management Settings: the Current course and Enrollment course selectors, with the Set up the next course link](../../assets/admin/admin-course-settings.png)

---

## Change the Current Course

1. Open **Current course** and pick the academic year from the list.
2. Save. The change takes effect immediately across the application — attendance sessions, new grade sessions, and incident tracking all switch to the selected year.

---

## Managing the courses

Each mark is moved with **its own selector** on this same page:

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Current course** | The day-to-day year: attendance, grades and incidents |
| **Enrollment default** | The year new enrollments are created for |

Both are unique: only one course can carry each.

### When to move the enrollment mark

During the year it points at the following one, which is where enrollments are being registered. **After the course transition it still points at the year that has just started**, because enrollments keep being processed in September for that year.

Move it on only when you open the next year's enrollment campaign. Just pick the course in the selector: the mark is removed from the previous one automatically.

### Creating a new course

If the year you need is not listed, type it in either selector and use **Create and edit…**: a form opens where you only fill in the start and end year. The name (`2029-2030`) is composed automatically.

> If you move this mark by accident, new enrollments are created for the wrong year and nothing warns you. Worth checking if you see enrollments turning up where they should not.

## Pre-enrollment period

Tutors only use **Academic management → Enrollment → Enrollment proposal** during pre-enrollment, so outside the **Pre-enrollment period** it is hidden from them. It is hidden as well from department and seminar chiefs, guidance and coexistence, who already have all of a student's information on the student's file. The academic administration, the secretary's office, the heads of studies, the deputy heads of studies and the director always see it.

1. In **Pre-enrollment period**, set the day, month and time it **Opens** and **Closes**, in the centre's local time. There is no year: the same dates repeat every year.
2. Save. The menu appears and disappears on its own at those moments.

By default it runs from 1 May at 08:00 to 31 July at 23:59. A tutor who opens the proposals from a saved link outside the period gets a message saying when it opens again.
