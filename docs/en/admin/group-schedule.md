# A Group's Weekly Schedule

See a group's full weekly timetable — subjects, teachers, classrooms and breaks — built
automatically from the schedules already set up for each teacher, from the group's own
record.

**Required role:** any staff role with access to Groups (Administrator, Secretary,
Teacher, Group Tutor, Head of Studies...) — everyone who can open a group can see its
schedule.

---

## Access

Navigate to: **Groups → [a group] → Schedule** tab

---

## Reading the Schedule

The group's weekly grid is not stored on its own — it is assembled from every teacher's
schedule that includes this group, so it is always up to date with whatever is set on the
**Employees → [teacher] → Schedule** tab. Each block shows:

- its exact start and end time (periods are not always aligned to the hour),
- the subject being taught,
- the break (patio), when the group's level and shift make it possible to work out its
  time — a reinforcement group, or one without a shift assigned, may simply not show a
  break block.

Below the grid, a **Subject → Teacher(s)** table lists every subject taught to this group
and who teaches it. When a subject is co-taught by more than one teacher at the same time,
all of their names appear together in that subject's row — the grid itself still shows that
subject only once, not once per teacher.

This tab is entirely read-only: to change anything, edit the relevant teacher's own
Schedule tab instead.

---

## Exporting the Schedule to PDF

Click **PDF** in the Schedule tab's toolbar to download a printable version of the group's
weekly timetable, including the Subject → Teacher(s) table.
