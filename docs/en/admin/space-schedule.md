# A Classroom's Occupation Schedule

See a classroom's full weekly occupation — every class booked into it, with subjects, teachers
and breaks — built automatically from the schedules already set up for each teacher, from the
room's own record. Always read-only.

**Required role:** any staff role with access to Spaces (Administrator, Secretary, Teacher) —
everyone who can open a space can see its occupation schedule.

---

## Access

Navigate to: **Educational Community → Configuration → Spaces → [a space] → Schedule** tab

---

## Reading the Schedule

The room's weekly grid is not stored on its own — it is assembled from every teacher's schedule
that books this room, so it is always up to date with whatever is set on the
**Employees → [teacher] → Schedule** tab. Each block shows:

- its exact start and end time (periods are not always aligned to the hour),
- the subject being taught — followed by its **topic**, when the teacher set one on their own
  Schedule tab,
- which teacher(s) teach it — more than one name for a co-taught class.

Unlike a group's or a student's own schedule, a room's grid always shows the **whole day**
(morning and afternoon together), since a classroom is routinely booked in both.

Below the grid, a **Subject → Teacher(s)** table lists every subject (and, when set, topic)
booked into this room and who teaches it.

Everything on this tab is read-only — to move a class to a different room, day or time, use the
relevant teacher's own Schedule tab, or the group's own Schedule tab if the change should apply
group-wide.

---

## Exporting the Schedule to PDF

Click **PDF** in the Schedule tab's toolbar to download a printable version of the classroom's
weekly occupation, including the Subject → Teacher(s) table.
