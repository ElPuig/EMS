# Resetting a Student's Google Password

When a student has lost the password of their Google Workspace account, you can give it a new one and generate its credentials.

**Required role:** Administrator or TAC coordinator

---

## Resetting the password

1. Open the student's form (**Educational Community → Students**).
2. In the header, click **Reset Google password**. The button only shows when the student's Google account is active.
3. Confirm the message.

![Student form header with the reset password button](../../assets/admin/credencials-google-03-restablir.png)

The previous password stops working straight away. The student must change the new one the first time they sign in.

---

## The new credentials

- A new **Google Workspace credentials** PDF appears in the form's **Documentation** tab. The previous one becomes **Cancelled**.
- If the student has a personal email, they also receive them by email.
- The form's chatter records who reset the password.

To download the PDF, click the file name in the **Documentation** tab. To download the credentials of several students at once, tick them in the **Students** list and use **Actions → Download Google credentials**.
