# Department and Seminar Chiefs — User Manuals

This section contains the manuals for **Department Chiefs** and **Seminar Chiefs**: what EMS lets you do for your department. Everything in the [teachers' manuals](../teachers/index.md) applies to you too.

---

## Available Manuals

| Manual | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| [Organising Absences](absences.md) | When a teacher of your department is away: sending guards, rearranging the lessons, telling the families about a timetable change, and entering a detected absence |
| [Notices: Writing to Your Department's Groups](notice.md) | Send notices to the students and families of the groups your department teaches |

## In the Teachers' Manuals

Some of what you do as a chief is explained in a teachers' manual, next to what every teacher does on the same screen:

| Manual | What concerns you as a chief |
|--------|-------------|
| [Meeting minutes](../teachers/minutes.md) | Writing your department's minutes, approving them, and approving in place of an absent chief |
| [Attendance: Reviewing Your Check-ins and Requesting Corrections](../teachers/attendance-corrections.md) | Consulting the check-ins of the people of your department (read-only) |
| [A Student's Public and Private Notes](../teachers/student-notes.md) | Who can read and write the private tutoring notes, a tutor's chief included |
| [Requesting an absence](../teachers/absences.md) | The email you receive when a teacher of your department requests an absence |
