# Erasmus+ team — User Manuals

This section contains the manuals for the **Erasmus+ team**: the Erasmus coordination and the mobility technician.

---

## Available Manuals

| Manual | Description |
|--------|------------|
| [Calls and applications](calls-and-applications.md) | Creating and publishing a call, reviewing the documents, asking for corrections and admitting or excluding applications |
| [Scoring and interviews](scoring-and-interviews.md) | The criteria of the call, the interviews with two evaluators and the ranking of each modality |
| [Resolutions, claims and place offers](resolutions-and-offers.md) | Publishing the admission list and the provisional and definitive resolutions, recording claims, offering the places and exporting the participants |
| [The staff call](staff-call.md) | The call for vocational training staff: the panel that rates the mobility plans, the staff criteria, one ranking for the whole call and the places |
| [Preparing the mobilities](mobility-preparation.md) | The centre's projects, destinations chosen by turns, own destinations, host entities, documents with their signers and deadlines, the commitment letter signed in EMS, the electronic signature of the agreements, the checklist and closing each mobility |
| [Group mobilities](group-mobilities.md) | Creating a group mobility, the students' enrolment from the portal, the ranking, approving the selection, withdrawals, the files and the closure |

---

## The Erasmus+ menu

| Menu | What you find there |
|------|---------------------|
| **Calls** | Calls, Applications, Ranking, Interviews, Plan ratings, Claims, Resolutions and Place offers |
| **Mobilities** | Individual mobilities and Group mobilities |
| **Financial management** | Projects, Invoices and Grant payments |
| **Configuration** | Host entities |

Each person only sees the menus that concern them: the Secretary, for example, only sees **Financial management**. The personal entries (**My evaluations**, **My plan ratings**, **My group mobilities**) are right on the bar, with no dropdown.
