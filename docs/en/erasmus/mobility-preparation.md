# Preparing the Erasmus+ mobilities

Once a place is accepted, prepare the mobility in EMS: the destination, the host entity and the dates, the documents to hand in before leaving and on return, and the steps to follow.

**Required role:** *Erasmus coordinator* or *Erasmus mobility technician* (Erasmus+ > Manager). The Head of Studies and the Director can read the mobilities; the Direction (the Director or the Head of Studies) validates the staff's activities (see [Validating Erasmus+ staff activities](../head_of_studies/erasmus-staff-validation.md)).

---

## 1. Before you start: the call

On the call, check these tabs:

- **Mobility documents:** what the participants hand in **before leaving** and **on return**. A new call already has the usual ones: for students, the European Health Insurance Card and, for minors, the family authorisation and the authorisation to travel abroad processed with the Mossos d'Esquadra, and the certificate of stay on return; for staff, the health card, the grant agreement, the mobility programme, the certificate of stay or attendance and the report with evidence. For each one, say whether it is required, if it is only for some modalities and **who signs it**: participant, legal guardian (minors only), centre and host entity. The documents are signed outside EMS and uploaded signed.
- **Checklist:** the steps that are not a document (preparation sessions, online language course, virtual component of GS, final report in the EU Survey, dissemination). You can limit a step to some modalities, for example the virtual component to GS.
- **Days to hand in the documents before leaving:** counted from the day each participant's destination is set (30 by default). While any is missing or needs changes, EMS emails them a reminder every week.
- **Days to hand in the return documents:** counted from the end of each mobility (30 by default).

**Students' call only**, on the **Destinations** tab: add each destination with its **modality**, a name, country, city, **places**, the **levels** it suits (empty: every level of the modality), the dates if they are known and notes for the students.

## 2. The destination choice (students)

When the definitive resolution is published and the destinations are ready, click **Actions > Open destination choice** on the call.

- A modality with **one destination**: every student who accepted a place is assigned to it directly.
- A modality with **several destinations**: the students choose on the portal **by turns, in rank order**. When their turn comes they receive an email and have the **Hours per choice turn** (72 by default) to choose among the destinations with free places that suit their level. When they choose, the next one gets the turn.
- Whoever does not answer in their turn **loses the turn, not the place**: the next one gets the turn, and they choose among the destinations left once everyone else has chosen (they receive another email then).
- Whoever finds that no destination suits them, or is not available for their dates, must talk to the Erasmus team to find an alternative: a destination they find themselves, or one through an intermediary organisation the centre works with. When it is agreed, write the country (and the city, host entity and dates you have) on their mobility and click **Actions > Set the agreed destination**.

A student who accepts a place later joins the queue in their rank order.

## 3. Own destinations (students)

A selected student can propose their own destination on the portal, with the host entity's written commitment. While it waits for validation, they don't get a turn.

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Mobilities** and use the **Proposal to validate** filter. You also get a to-do.
2. Open the mobility and check the **Proposal** tab: host entity, contact, country, city, dates and the commitment.
3. Click **Actions > Validate the proposal**. To reject it, write the **Reason of the decision** first and click **Actions > Reject the proposal**: the student receives it by email and goes back to choosing among the centre's destinations.

## 4. Prepare each mobility

Open the mobility from **Erasmus+ > Mobilities** (or the **Mobilities** button of the call):

- Fill in the **Host entity** (a company contact, which stays for next years) and its **Host contact person**, and correct the place and the **dates** if needed.
- **Documents** tab: the participant uploads them on the portal, and you can also attach them here. Mark each one **Valid** or **Needs changes** (with a comment the participant sees). A new file puts it back to review; earlier files are kept. **Ready to leave** is ticked when every required document before leaving is valid and every step before leaving is done.
- **Checklist** tab: tick each step when it is done. EMS records who ticked it and when.
- **Staff:** the staff member completes their proposal with the exact dates and the Direction validates it. Then they request their **absence for training** for those days from the portal, with their responsible declaration, and whoever approves their absences approves it as usual. If the dates change before it is approved, the absence follows them; if it was already approved, EMS warns you on the mobility to correct it by hand.
- **Actions > Notify pending documents** emails the participant the documents missing or needing changes.
- For staff, if the documents depend on the modality, assign the modality on the application and click **Actions > Update documents and checklist** (it is done on its own when you change it on the application). Use it too after adding documents or steps to the call.

## 5. Return and closing

When the participant is back and every **return** document is valid and every return step is done (**Return complete**), click **Actions > Close the mobility**.

To cancel a mobility (for example, the travel documents were not handed in on time), write the **Reason for cancelling** on the **Cancellation** tab and click **Actions > Cancel the mobility**. The application is marked as renounced and the place becomes free. The participant can also renounce on the portal.

> **Graduates:** a student who graduates and does the mobility in the summer keeps access to the portal after the course transition, until the mobility is closed or cancelled. Then EMS removes the access, as the course transition would have done.
