# Preparing the Erasmus+ mobilities

Once a place is accepted, prepare the mobility in EMS: the destination, the host entity and the dates, the documents to hand in before leaving and on return, and the steps to follow.

**Required role:** *Erasmus coordinator* or *Erasmus mobility technician* (Erasmus+ > Manager). The Head of Studies and the Director can read the mobilities; the Direction (the Director or the Head of Studies) validates the staff's activities (see [Validating Erasmus+ staff activities](../head_of_studies/erasmus-staff-validation.md)).

---

## 1. Before you start: the projects and the call

In **Erasmus+ > Projects**, keep the centre's active Erasmus+ projects: their agreement number and kind (**KA121-VET** for CFGM, **KA131-HED** for CFGS). Several of the same kind can be active at once (one per call year); archive a project when it ends.

On the call, check these tabs:

- **Mobility documents:** what the participants hand in **before leaving** and **on return**. EMS already has their identity document, bank account, personal details and emergency contact, so they are not asked for. A new call already has the usual ones: for students, the European Health Insurance Card (as soon as they have it), the OLS certificate with their English level, the inclusion documentation if it applies (optional and confidential), for minors the family authorisation and the authorisation to travel abroad processed with the Mossos d'Esquadra, the grant agreement, the learning agreement and the insurance policy, and the certificate of stay on return; for staff, the health card, the grant agreement, the insurance policy, the mobility programme, the certificate of stay or attendance and the report with evidence. For each one, say whether it is required, if it is only for some modalities, **who signs it** (participant, legal guardian for minors only, centre, host entity), its deadline (**Due**: after the destination is set, before leaving or after returning, and **Days**) and **Handled by**: *the participant uploads it*; *the team fills it in, the participant signs it* (the grant agreement and the learning agreement: you upload them filled in, the participant downloads, signs and uploads them, and is not asked for them before); or *the team provides it* (the insurance policy: you upload it and the participant only downloads it). By default, the travel documents are due a month after the destination is set; the agreements a month before leaving at the latest (ideally three); the OLS certificate and the insurance policy a week before leaving; and the return documents a month after returning. The documents are signed outside EMS and uploaded signed.
- **Commitment letter** (students): the text of annex 3, already filled in on a new call. Each selected student reads it on the portal, ticks that they accept it and clicks **Accept and sign**: EMS keeps the text accepted, who signed it, when and from which IP address. Leave it empty if the call asks for none.
- **Checklist:** the steps that are not a document (preparation sessions, virtual component of GS, final report in the EU Survey, dissemination). You can limit a step to some modalities, for example the virtual component to GS.

**Students' call only**, on the **Destinations** tab: add each destination with its **modality**, a name, country, city, **places**, the **levels** it suits (empty: every level of the modality), the dates if they are known and notes for the students.

## 2. The destination choice (students)

When the definitive resolution is published and the destinations are ready, click **Actions > Open destination choice** on the call.

- A modality with **one destination**: every student who accepted a place is assigned to it directly.
- A modality with **several destinations**: the students choose on the portal **by turns, in rank order**. When their turn comes they receive an email and have the **Hours per choice turn** (72 by default) to choose among the destinations with free places that suit their level. When they choose, the next one gets the turn.
- Whoever does not answer in their turn **loses the turn, not the place**: the next one gets the turn, and they choose among the destinations left once everyone else has chosen (they receive another email then).
- Whoever finds that no destination suits them, or is not available for their dates, must talk to the Erasmus team to find an alternative: a destination they find themselves, or one through an intermediary organisation the centre works with. When it is agreed, write the country (and the city, host entity and dates you have) on their mobility and click **Actions > Set the agreed destination**.

A student who accepts a place later joins the queue in their rank order.

## 3. Own destinations (students)

A selected student can propose their own destination on the portal, with the host entity's written commitment. While it waits for validation, they don't get a turn.

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Mobilities** and use the **Proposal to validate** filter. You also get a to-do.
2. Open the mobility and check the **Proposal** tab: host entity, contact, country, city, dates and the commitment.
3. Click **Actions > Validate the proposal**. To reject it, write the **Reason of the decision** first and click **Actions > Reject the proposal**: the student receives it by email and goes back to choosing among the centre's destinations.

## 4. Prepare each mobility

Open the mobility from **Erasmus+ > Mobilities** (or the **Mobilities** button of the call):

- Fill in the **Host entity** (a company contact, which stays for next years) and its **Host contact person**, correct the place and the **dates** if needed, and choose the **Project** the mobility goes on: EMS only offers the projects of the modality's kind (CFGM on KA121-VET, CFGS on KA131-HED).
- **Documents** tab: in **Files from the team**, attach the document filled in for the participant to sign, or the one you provide (the insurance policy). The participant downloads it on the portal. The participant uploads the others on the portal, and you can also attach them here. Each one shows its **Deadline**. Mark each one **Valid** or **Needs changes** (with a comment the participant sees). A new file puts it back to review; earlier files are kept. **Ready to leave** is ticked when every required document before leaving is valid (or, if the team provides it, uploaded), every step before leaving is done and the commitment letter is signed (**Commitment letter** tab). While anything is missing, EMS reminds the participant by email every week.
- **Checklist** tab: tick each step when it is done. EMS records who ticked it and when.
- **Staff:** the staff member completes their proposal with the exact dates and their responsible declaration, and the Direction validates it. Then EMS requests their **absence for training** for those days on its own, and whoever approves their absences approves it as usual. If the dates change before it is approved, the absence follows them; if it was already approved, EMS warns you on the mobility to correct it by hand.
- **Actions > Notify pending documents** emails the participant the documents missing or needing changes.
- For staff, if the documents depend on the modality, assign the modality on the application and click **Actions > Update documents and checklist** (it is done on its own when you change it on the application). Use it too after adding documents or steps to the call.

## 5. Return and closing

When the participant is back and every **return** document is valid and every return step is done (**Return complete**), click **Actions > Close the mobility**.

To cancel a mobility (for example, the travel documents were not handed in on time), write the **Reason for cancelling** on the **Cancellation** tab and click **Actions > Cancel the mobility**. The application is marked as renounced and the place becomes free. The participant can also renounce on the portal.

> **Graduates:** a student who graduates and does the mobility in the summer keeps access to the portal after the course transition, until the mobility is closed or cancelled. Then EMS removes the access, as the course transition would have done.
