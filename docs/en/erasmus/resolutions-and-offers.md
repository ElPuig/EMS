# Erasmus+ resolutions, claims and place offers

Prepare and publish the call's lists, record the claims, and offer the places until they are all accepted.

**Required role:** *Erasmus coordinator* or *Erasmus mobility technician* (Erasmus+ > Manager). Only the Erasmus coordinator (Erasmus+ > Coordinator) can publish a resolution: publishing it is approving it. The Head of Studies and the Director can read resolutions, offers and claims.

---

## 1. Before you start

On the call, fill in:

- **Claims deadline:** until when students can claim against the provisional resolution. They see it on the portal and in the email.
- **Acceptance deadline:** until when the selected students can accept their place. Needed to publish the definitive resolution.

Every applicant needs their **identity document** (DNI, NIE or passport) on their contact: the published lists identify people only by four of its digits, never by name.

## 2. The resolutions, in order

| Resolution | When | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Admission list | After closing the call and admitting or excluding every application | Admitted and excluded |
| Provisional resolution | When every admitted application has a complete score and no draw is pending | Rank, score and provisional result |
| Definitive resolution | Once the claims deadline has passed and every claim is answered | Rank, score and final result: selected, reserve or not selected |
| Rectification | Whenever a published resolution must be corrected | The corrected ranking |

Each one needs the previous one published. A published resolution can never be changed or deleted: to correct it, publish a rectification.

## 3. Prepare and publish a resolution

1. On the call, open the **Resolutions** tab (or **Erasmus+ > Calls > Resolutions**) and add a new one: choose its **Kind**.
2. Click **Actions > Prepare lines**. EMS freezes the list as the applications are now. If something is missing (an application not decided, a score incomplete, an identity document), EMS tells you who. You can prepare it again as many times as you need while it is a draft.
3. For a rectification, explain in **Notes for applicants** what it corrects. The notes are published with the list and emailed to everyone on it: never write anyone's name.
4. The Erasmus coordinator reviews the list and clicks **Actions > Publish**. EMS keeps who published it and when, and the published list shows that date as the approval date.

When it is published:

- Every applicant on it receives their result by email and sees it on the portal, with the detail of their score.
- The list is published on the portal without names. To post it on the centre's website, click **Actions > Print the published list** on the resolution to get the PDF (from the list of resolutions, **Print > Published list**).
- With the **provisional** resolution, every interview of the call is locked: only the Erasmus team can reopen one, with a rectification note.
- With the **definitive** resolution (or a rectification), each application gets its result. The selected get a place offer with the acceptance deadline: the result email already asks them to accept it on the portal, and the family of a minor gets a copy. The email and the portal also say that the list can be appealed before the Head of Studies within a month. A reserve's email gives their number on the reserve list of their modality. On the call, the **Places accepted** button shows how many places are accepted out of the total, and a notice says how many offers are still waiting for an answer: when none is left, you can open the destination choice.

> **Who is selected:** the eligible applications (at least the minimum score) within the places of their modality. The rest of the eligible ones are on the reserve list, in order. Below the minimum score: not selected.

In the resolution, the lines are coloured: green, admitted or selected; orange, reserve; red, excluded; black, not selected. An excluded line has no rank nor score, and the **Exclusion reason** column shows why (only to the Erasmus team: the published list and its PDF never show it; each applicant reads their own reason in the result email and on the portal). Applicants see their own row highlighted in the published list on the portal (never in the PDF).

## 4. Claims and appeals

Applicants claim from the portal, under their provisional result, until the claims deadline: EMS records the claim with its files and the moment it arrived, and everyone in the Erasmus team gets a task to answer it, marked done when it is resolved. A claim that arrives by email is recorded by hand in **Erasmus+ > Calls > Claims** (or on the **Results** tab of the application):

1. **Application**, **Against** (the resolution), **Kind** (claim or appeal before the Head of Studies), **Received on**, **What is claimed** and the files.
2. Choose the **Result** (*Upheld*, *Partially upheld* or *Dismissed*), write the **Response** and click **Actions > Resolve**. It cannot be resolved without both.

The applicant receives an email when the claim is recorded (their proof that it arrived in time) and another one with the result and the response, which they also see on the portal, under **My claims**.

**Appeals against the definitive resolution** (within a month of its publication) are answered by the applicant's own Head of Studies, not by the Erasmus team: the Head or Deputy Head of Studies above the student's group tutor (above the teacher themself in a staff call; the Director if the applicant is that Head of Studies). Record the appeal as above with **Kind** *Appeal before the Head of Studies*: EMS gives them a task, and they choose the **Result**, write the **Response** and resolve it. The Erasmus team sees the appeal but cannot answer it.

If a claim is upheld, correct the application's data (for example the language certificate) before preparing the definitive resolution. EMS does not let you prepare the definitive resolution before the **Claims deadline** or while a claim is still open. After the definitive one, publish a rectification.

## 5. Place offers

**Erasmus+ > Calls > Place offers** lists every offer and its answer: *Waiting for an answer*, *Place accepted*, *Renounced*, *No answer* or *Withdrawn*. On the pending ones, **Time left** says how many days are left to answer.

- Students accept or renounce on the portal before the deadline, and receive an email confirming their answer (with a copy to the family of a minor). Without an answer by then, EMS marks the place as renounced and tells the student by email that the deadline passed; the portal says they did not answer in time, not that they renounced.
- When a student accepts, their group tutor gets a notice (not a task) saying they will go on an Erasmus+ stay.
- When a place becomes free and there is a reserve, everyone in the Erasmus team gets a task on the call (*Erasmus+: free place in ...*). It is marked done once the place is offered. **EMS never offers it on its own**. On the call, the **Resolutions** tab shows the free places of each modality and the **Next reserve**. Click **Actions > Offer a free place**: check the proposed student and the **Answer before** deadline (it comes with as long as the definitive offers had, or three days; change it if needed) and click **Offer the place**. The student receives an email.
- To withdraw an offer still waiting (for example, made by mistake), click **Withdraw** on it. The answer's date is an optional column, and the colour is in the status label, not in the row.

A rectification never undoes an offer already accepted or renounced: those students are told they keep the place they accepted (or that they had renounced). If a student with an offer still waiting is no longer selected after the rectification, EMS withdraws that offer and the place goes to whoever the rectification selects.

Because an accepted place is never withdrawn, a rectification can leave more people with a place than places. EMS then shows a warning on the rectification and does not let you publish it until the Erasmus team decides what to do, for example adding a place to the modality on the call.

## 6. Export the participants

Once places have been accepted, click **Actions > Export to spreadsheet** on the call (or select applications in the list). You get an Excel file with one row per student who accepted a place: personal data, cycle and year, bank account and, for minors, their legal guardians. Only the Erasmus team can download it, and every download is recorded (who, when and how many people) in the call's history.

> **Careful:** the file contains personal and bank data. Keep it only where it is needed.
