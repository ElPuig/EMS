# Erasmus+ scoring and interviews

Score the admitted applications with the criteria of art. 12 of the call, organise the interviews with two evaluators and get the ranking of each modality.

**Required role:** *Erasmus coordinator* or *Erasmus mobility technician* (Erasmus+ > Manager).

---

## 1. Close the call: EMS freezes the data

When the application period is over, click **Actions > Close applications** on the call. At that moment EMS saves, for every submitted application:

- **A. Record average:** every module of the student's current cycle, all its years. Past years count with the final grade; this year's modules with the provisional grade of the learning outcomes evaluated so far. A module repeated counts once, with its latest grade. Convalidated modules and modules with nothing evaluated yet are left out. The average is weighted by the hours of each module.
- **F. Conduct warnings:** the student's warnings in this cycle up to the closing date. EMS only has the warnings recorded since it started being used.

Later changes in grades or warnings do not change these values. You see them on the **Scoring** tab of each application, with the list of modules used.

> **Tip:** close the call right after the closing date, so the data is the one EMS had on that date.

A student who joined the centre this year only has this year's modules: their average is the provisional grade of the learning outcomes already graded. If a student has no learning outcome graded at all, the average stays *pending* and the application has no complete score.

## 2. Fill in the criteria checked by the team

On the **Scoring** tab of each admitted application:

| Criterion | Field | Points |
|---|---|---|
| C. Languages | **Language certificate**: the highest level the student uploaded | B1 2.5, B2 5, C1 7.5, C2 10 |
| D. Fewer opportunities | **Fewer opportunities accredited** (only the Erasmus team sees it) | 10 or 0 |
| E. First mobility | **First mobility** | 5 or 0 |

Every criterion starts empty. **An empty criterion is never zero:** the application stays without a complete score and out of the ranking until it is filled in. EMS records who filled in each one and when.

## 3. Organise the interviews

On the **Interview** tab of each admitted application:

1. Fill in the **Interview date** and **Interview place**.
2. In the list, add the **two evaluators** (anyone of the staff). Each evaluator gets a task in EMS and sees the interview in **Erasmus+ > My evaluations**. They only see their own interview, not the other evaluator's score nor the rest of the application.
3. Click **Actions > Send interview call**. The student receives an email with the date and place. It must be sent at least 24 hours before the interview.

Each evaluator scores the 7 criteria of the rubric (annex 4) from 0 to 5 and submits. The interview score is the average of both evaluators. If only one evaluator could score it for a justified reason, write it in **Reason for a single evaluation**.

On the call, the **Score correction deadline** sets until when the evaluators can reopen and change their own evaluation. After it, only the Erasmus team can reopen an interview, from **Erasmus+ > Interviews**, writing a **Rectification note**.

If a student does not attend the interview, write the reason and click **Actions > Exclude**.

## 4. The ranking

Click **Ranking** on the call (or **Erasmus+ > Ranking**): the admitted applications of each modality in order, with the score of each criterion.

- A modality is only ranked when **every** admitted application in it has a complete score. Until then the rank is 0.
- Ties are broken, in this order, by fewer opportunities, first mobility, the English part of the interview and the record average.
- If two applications are still tied, they are marked **Needs a draw**. The Erasmus coordinator clicks **Actions > Draw the ties** on the call (Resolutions tab): EMS orders each tied set at random and keeps the draw (who, when, the applications tied and the order drawn), which nobody can change. The order is in **Draw** (the lowest number goes first). If a correction (for example, a claim upheld) ties someone with applications already drawn, they are marked **Needs a draw** again and the whole tie is drawn anew; both draws are kept.
- Applications below the call's **Minimum score** (50 by default) are shown in red: they are not eligible.
