# Enrolling in an Erasmus+ group mobility

Some Erasmus+ mobilities are **group** ones: a group of students goes with two accompanying teachers. If there is one for your group, you will see it on the portal, on the **Erasmus+** page.

---

## Enrolling

1. Log in to the portal and go to **Erasmus+**.
2. The group mobility's card shows the project, the destination and dates (once confirmed), the places and the enrolment period. Open **Selection criteria** to see how the selection is made.
3. Click **Enrol me**. If you are in a situation of fewer opportunities, you can tick it (it is optional and only the Erasmus team sees it).
4. While the enrolment is open you can click **Withdraw my enrolment** and enrol again.

No CV, letter nor interview is needed. If you have as many conduct warnings this course as the limit that alerts the coexistence team, you cannot enrol.

## The result

First the Erasmus coordination publishes a **provisional list**: you receive an email with your provisional result and, on the portal's card, you see it and the link to the list. The list identifies each student only by four digits of their identity document. If you disagree, reply to the email before the claims deadline shown.

Then the coordination publishes the **definitive list** and you receive an email with your result (selected, on the reserve list with your number, or not meeting the requirements). You also see it on the portal's card, with the link to both lists. You only see your own result.

## If you are selected

Click **See my documents**: you will find the documents you have to hand in (for example the European Health Insurance Card, the signed grant agreement or, if you are a minor, the official authorisation to travel abroad), who signs them (and whether the Erasmus team has already checked each signature) and their deadlines, and the group's documents the Erasmus team adds. Upload each file and click **Upload**.

On the same page you will find the **commitment letter** of the mobility (destination and dates, taking part in the activities, the coexistence rules and the teachers' instructions, what happens after a serious breach, and keeping in touch with the centre). Read it, tick that you accept it and click **Accept and sign**. If you are a minor, your family has to accept it too: it receives an email and finds the letter on its own portal, under **Erasmus+**. If the destination or the dates are not confirmed yet, the acceptance is provisional: once they are, or if the conditions change later (destination, dates, travel or teachers), you (and your family, if you are a minor) accept the new letter. The earlier acceptance is kept. The letter does not replace the official authorisation for minors to travel abroad, which you hand in as a document. If you cannot go, open **I cannot go: renounce the place** as soon as possible, so that the place goes to the next reserve.
