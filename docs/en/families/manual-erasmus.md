# Applying for an Erasmus+ internship

Apply for an Erasmus+ internship abroad and follow your application from the student portal.

**Who:** students of a vocational training cycle (CFGM or CFGS), any year, while a call is open.

---

## 1. Open the Erasmus+ page

In the portal, click the **Erasmus+** card. It only appears when there is a call you can apply to, or when you have already applied.

Each call shows its dates and a link to **Read the call**. Read it before applying: it explains the requirements, the documents and how the places are awarded.

If you have 3 or more conduct warnings this course, you cannot apply (article 6 of the call): instead of the **Apply** button you will see a notice. If you think it is a mistake, talk to the Erasmus team.

## 2. Fill in the application

Click **Apply** and fill in the form:

- Whether you have already taken part in an Erasmus+ mobility, and which one.
- Your availability for the periods of the call.
- Whether you can prove a situation of fewer opportunities. The evidence is confidential: only the Erasmus team sees it.
- Whether you consent to the use of your image (voluntary).

Then attach the documents. The ones marked **Required** are mandatory. You can attach several files to each document.

Click **Save draft** to continue later.

## 3. Submit it

Click **Submit application**. You can only submit it when every required document is attached. You will receive a receipt by email with the files received.

Until the closing date you can still change it and submit it again: each time you will receive a new receipt. After the closing date the application can no longer be changed.

## 4. If you are asked for corrections

If a document is missing or needs changes, you will receive an email with the list and the deadline. Open your application on the portal: the documents to correct are marked in red. Attach the new files and click **Submit application** again. If you don't correct it in time, the application will be excluded.

## 5. The interview

If your application is admitted, you will receive an email with the date and place of your personal interview, at least 24 hours ahead. You will also see them on your application in the portal. Part of the interview is in English. Arrive 15 minutes early: not attending excludes you, unless for a justified reason of force majeure.

## 6. Your results

When the Erasmus+ Commission publishes a list (the admission list, the provisional resolution, the definitive resolution), you receive an email with your result and you see it on your application in the portal, under **Results**: your position in your modality, your total score and the points of each criterion.

Click **See the published list** to see the whole list. It does not show names: each person appears only with four digits of their identity document, for example ***4567**. You can also download it as a PDF.

If you disagree with the provisional result, you can claim by replying to the result email before the claims deadline shown with it.

## 7. Accept or renounce your place

If you are **selected** in the definitive resolution, your application shows the place offered and the deadline to answer:

- Click **I accept the place** to accept it.
- If you cannot go, open **I want to renounce the place**, write the reason if you want, and click **Confirm that I renounce the place**.

Without an answer by the deadline, the place is considered renounced. If you are on the **reserve list** and a place becomes free, you will receive an email and the offer will appear on your application, with its own deadline.

## 8. Prepare your mobility

When you accept the place, your application shows **Your mobility**.

**Choose your destination.** If your modality has several destinations, the selected choose in order of the list, starting with the first one. When it is your turn you receive an email: open your application, pick one of the destinations with free places for your cycle and click **Choose this destination** before the deadline shown. If you don't answer in time, you don't lose the place, but you lose your turn: you will choose among the destinations left once everyone else has chosen. If none of them suits you, or you are not available for their dates, talk to the Erasmus team (erasmus@elpuig.xeill.net) to find an alternative: a destination you find yourself, or one through an organisation the centre works with. If your modality has only one destination, you are assigned to it directly.

**Your own destination.** You can also find a company or entity in another Erasmus+ country yourself. Open **Propose my own destination**, fill in the entity, its contact person, the country, the city and the dates, attach its **written commitment** to take you, and click **Send the proposal**. The entity must also write to erasmus@elpuig.xeill.net. The Erasmus team validates it; if not, you will receive the reason and you can choose among the centre's destinations.

**Documents and steps.** Once you have a destination, the card shows the place, the host entity and the dates, and:

- The **documents** to hand in before leaving and on return, who must **sign** each one and its deadline. Your identity document, bank account and personal details are already in EMS. Upload the European Health Insurance Card (both sides) as soon as you have it, and, if you are a minor, your family's authorisation and the authorisation to travel abroad processed with the Mossos d'Esquadra: within a month of getting your destination, and the OLS certificate with your English level in the same time. If it applies to you, the inclusion documentation (fewer opportunities, grants, special needs): only the Erasmus team sees it. In a higher-education (GS) stay of 30 days or fewer, also the **virtual component**: a PDF with your preparation, which you can upload from a month before leaving until the day before (information about the city, how you will get to the company and how much transport costs, and the exercises on the technologies and tasks of the host company). The grant agreement and the learning agreement are uploaded filled in by the Erasmus team: download them, sign them and upload them signed (ideally three months before leaving, a month before at the latest). The Erasmus team also uploads your insurance policy, for you to download, and the certificate of stay: download it, have your tutor at the host company sign it at the end of the stay and upload it signed within a month of your return. While anything is missing you will receive a reminder every week. Sign the documents outside EMS, attach the signed copy and click **Upload the documents**. If the Erasmus team asks for a change, you will see its comment in red.
- The **commitment letter** (annex 3 of the call): read it on the portal, tick that you accept it and click **Accept and sign**. EMS keeps it signed. If you are a minor, your family signs it too: it receives an email and signs it from its own access to the portal, in **Erasmus+**.
- The **steps** to follow (preparation sessions, the online language course, the final report...). The Erasmus team ticks them when they are done.

**During the stay.** Every week, answer the **weekly self-assessment** on the card: what you did, what you learned, how you rate your week from 1 to 5 and what was hardest for you. Each week can be answered from its first day, and only once. If you forget one, you will receive a reminder. The Erasmus team reads it and it is kept in your record.

If you have to renounce, open **I want to renounce the place**. Renouncing without a justified cause may mean paying the expenses already committed (tickets, bookings).

If you graduate this course and do the mobility in the summer, you keep access to the portal until it ends.

## 9. Follow your application

The Erasmus+ page shows the state of your application: *Draft*, *Submitted*, *Corrections requested*, *Admitted*, *Excluded*, *Selected*, *Reserve*, *Not selected*, *Place accepted* or *Renounced*, and the state of your mobility: *Defining the mobility*, *Preparing*, *Closed* or *Cancelled*.

For any question, write to the Erasmus coordination: **erasmus@elpuig.xeill.net**.
