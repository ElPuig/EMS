# Checking the Student's Class Schedule

See the student's weekly class schedule — subjects, classrooms, breaks and teachers — and
download it as a PDF, from the student portal.

---

## Access

On the portal's home page, click the **Attendance** card.

If you are a family with more than one child at the school, first choose the child in the
portal's top bar: the schedule shown is always the one for the selected child.

---

## Reading the Schedule

The grid shows one column per day, from Monday to Friday, and one row per class period. Each
block shows the subject and the classroom. Below the grid, a table lists each subject and the
teacher or teachers who teach it.

![Student's weekly schedule on the portal, with the Download PDF button](../../assets/families/manual-horari-01.png)

On a mobile phone, slide the grid sideways to see every day.

---

## Downloading the Schedule as PDF

Click **Download PDF** to save a printable copy of the same schedule.
