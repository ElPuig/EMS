# Erasmus+: checking the projects' economy

The Director can check the economy of every Erasmus+ project, the current ones and those of earlier calls, without changing anything: the Erasmus coordination manages it and the Secretary pays.

**Required role:** Director.

---

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Projects**. Each row is a project: *Awarded* (the grant with its amendments), *Committed* (the confirmed mobilities' grants and the project's other expenses), *Paid*, *Available* (grant minus committed; it is not the money received from SEPIE) and *Support generated* (the organisational support of the completed mobilities). The **pivot** view compares calls and actions; **Archived too** shows the finished projects.
2. Open a project to see its **Economy** tab: the **Subsidy** and **Budget** blocks, the grant amendments, SEPIE's transfers, the organisational support and the warnings (for example, commitments above the grant or expenses without a receipt).
3. The project's **Services paid by the centre** and **Project expenses** tabs, and the **Financial management** menus (**Grant payments**, **Services paid by the centre**, **Project expenses**), list what has been paid, without the participants' bank accounts.
4. **Actions > Download the economy (Excel)** downloads the project's economic report, with the same figures as the projects list.
