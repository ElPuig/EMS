# Erasmus+: checking the projects' economy

The Director can check the economy of every Erasmus+ project, the current ones and those of earlier calls, without changing anything: the Erasmus coordination manages it and the Secretary pays.

**Required role:** Director.

---

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Financial management > Economic panel**. Each row is a project: *Awarded* (the grant with its amendments), *Committed* (the confirmed mobilities' grants and the project's other expenses), *Paid*, *Available* (grant minus committed; it is not the money received from SEPIE) and *Support generated* (the organisational support of the completed mobilities). The **pivot** view compares calls and actions; **Archived too** shows the finished projects.
2. Open a project to see its **Economy** tab: the grant amendments, SEPIE's transfers, the grant agreement and the economic documents, the expenses, the organisational support and the warnings (for example, commitments above the grant or expenses without a receipt).
3. The **Invoices** and **Grant payments** menus list what has been paid, without the participants' bank accounts.
4. **Actions > Download the economy (Excel)** downloads the project's economic report, with the same figures as the panel.
