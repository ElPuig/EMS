# Erasmus+: checking serious sanctions

The Erasmus+ call excludes students with a sanction for conduct seriously harmful to coexistence during the current course (for example, an expulsion). EMS has no structured record of those sanctions, so the Head of Studies or the Director records the check by hand for each application.

**Required role:** Head of Studies, Deputy Head of Studies or Director.

---

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Applications** and use the **Sanction check pending** filter.
2. Open an application and go to the **Admission** tab.
3. In **No serious sanction**, choose *Met* (no serious sanction) or *Not met*, and write a **Sanction check note** with the reference you checked.
4. Save. EMS records who made the check and when.

You can open the applications and their non-confidential documents, but you can only change this check: the rest of the application is managed by the Erasmus team.

You can also type the record average of a student with no grades in EMS, from their certificate of previous studies: see [Erasmus+: typing a record average](../secretary/erasmus-record-average.md).
