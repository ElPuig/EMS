# Erasmus+: validating the staff's activities

The staff call says that the Direction validates the activity, the host entity or course, the destination and the dates of every selected staff member before the mobility is formalised, taking into account the centre's organisation, the cover of the classes, the project's objectives and the budget.

**Required role:** Director or Head of Studies (either of them).

---

1. When a staff member sends their proposal with the exact dates, you get a to-do. Go to **Erasmus+ > Mobilities** and use the **Proposal to validate** filter.
2. Open the mobility and check the **Proposal** tab: activity, host entity or course, contact, country, city, dates and notes.
3. To validate it, click **Actions > Validate the proposal**. The mobility moves to *Preparing* with those dates, the Erasmus team prepares it with the staff member, and EMS requests their absence for training for those days, with the responsible declaration they gave with the proposal.
4. If you don't validate it, write the **Reason of the decision** first and click **Actions > Reject the proposal**. The staff member receives the reason by email and proposes another activity on the portal; you get a new to-do.

EMS records who decided and when. You can read the rest of the mobility, but the Erasmus team manages it.
