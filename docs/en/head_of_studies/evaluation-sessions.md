# Evaluation sessions: creating, publishing and closing them

This guide explains how the Head of Studies (and the Deputy Head of Studies) manages each evaluation round: creating its grade sessions, moving them through their states and publishing the grades to students and families.

## Access

**Planning and Grading → Grades → Evaluation management**:

- **Create grade sessions**
- **Change grade session state**

The sessions themselves are in **Planning and Grading → Grades → Evaluation by group and subject**.

---

## Creating the sessions of a round

1. Open **Create grade sessions**.
2. Choose the **Mode**: **By study** (one or more studies) or **By level** (every study of a level), and select them.
3. **Round** is filled in for you: the next round after the last one created for those studies. The fourth one is the **extraordinary evaluation**.
4. Fill in **Claims from** and **Claims until**: the days students may claim a grade once it is published. Both are required.
5. Press **Create sessions**.

EMS creates one session per group and subject with enrolled students (tutorships aside), each one filled with its students and their learning outcomes, and shows you the list of the sessions created.

---

## Changing the claims period or the provisional grades

Each session keeps its **Claims from** and **Claims until** dates and the **Provisional grades allowed** box (ticked by default: teachers may mark a learning outcome's grade as provisional).

- **One session:** change them on the session's form.
- **Several sessions at once:** in the sessions' list, select them, change the value in one of the selected rows and confirm: it is applied to all of them.

Only the Head of Studies and the administration can change them.

---

## The states of a session

| State | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Open** | The session's teacher and the tutor enter the grades. |
| **Board** | Evaluation board: only the tutor (and the administration) can modify the grades. |
| **Published** | Students and families see the grades in the portal, and the claims period is open. Only the administration can modify them. |
| **Finalised** | The round is closed. Only the administration can modify the grades. |

A session moves **one step at a time**, forwards or backwards: from Open to Board, from Board back to Open or on to Published, and so on.

---

## Changing the state

**For the sessions you choose:** in the sessions' list, select them and use **Actions → Change state**. On a session's form, the same option is in its **Actions** menu.

**For a whole study or level:** open **Change grade session state**, choose the studies or levels and the round.

In both cases the **Target state** only offers the states the sessions can move to, the next one by default. Press **Apply**.

---

## Publishing the grades

When the target state is **Published**:

- The sessions need their claims period: without it, they cannot be published.
- If any of them has **no learning outcome graded at all**, a warning lists them (a whole module may have been forgotten). You can publish them anyway.
- Tick **Announce the publication** to email every student, and the family of the minors, that the grades are in the portal. They get one email per evaluation, with the claims period and a button to the portal; the email never carries the grades.

From then on, students and families see the grades in the portal's **Grades** card and can download them as a non-official PDF (see the [student portal guide](../families/manual-portal-alumne.md#checking-the-grades)).
