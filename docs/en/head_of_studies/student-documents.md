# Consulting student documents

This guide explains how the Head of Studies, the Deputy Head of Studies and the Director consult the documents that students and families submit: ID cards, medical cards, IBANs, supporting documents for bonifications and exemptions, and the Google credentials.

---

## Which documents you see

You see every document of the students whose tutor is in your area: the tutors who are below you in the centre's hierarchy. The Director sees those of every student with a tutor. You don't see the documents of students from another Head of Studies' area.

You can only consult them. Approving, rejecting or reopening a document is the secretary's office's job (see [Reviewing student documents](../secretary/student-documents.md)).

## Where to consult them

- **Academic management > Student Documents**: the list of documents. It opens showing only the **Pending** ones; remove the filter to see them all. Click a row to open the document and download its file.
- On a student's record, **Secretary** tab, **Documentation** section: the same documents, for that student only.
