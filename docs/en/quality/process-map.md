# Process map and procedures

The centre's process map lives in **Quality → Configuration → Processes**. Each process carries its code
(`PE1`, `PC2`, `PS1`...), its kind (strategic, key or support) and the **post** responsible for it.

**Why a post and not a person:** the map says "Director" or "Quality coordinator", not a name. When
somebody else takes the post over in September the map is still right without touching anything, and the
record shows you alongside who currently holds it.

**Who has access:** quality coordination and the management team can edit it; the other posts can read
it.

---

## A process record

It holds the code, the name, the kind, the responsible post, whether it is a **quality process** (the
question the management review asks) and when its SWOT was last reviewed.

Below there are two tabs:

- **Procedures** — the procedures hanging off the process. They can be added from here.
- **Documents** — every controlled document of the process, with its version and state.

## A procedure record

In **Quality → Configuration → Procedures**, or opened from its process. It holds what the centre's own
procedure sheet already states:

- **What**, **What for** and **Who it applies to** — the three opening blocks.
- **Phases** — the steps of the procedure and the tools each is carried out with. They can be reordered
  by dragging.
- **Documents and records** — what the procedure produces and what it uses.

## Worth checking once a year

- That every process has its **responsible post** filled in, and that the post is actually held.
- That the **SWOT review date** is not two years old.
- In the document registry, the **Code out of the current map** facet: any document showing up there is
  quoting a process that no longer exists.
