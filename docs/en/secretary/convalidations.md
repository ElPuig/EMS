# Convalidations: registering resolutions

Register resolved convalidations in Esfera and close them in EMS, so the student receives the resolution and sees the grade.

**Required role:** Secretariat.

---

## When it is your turn

A request reaches the secretariat once it is resolved, in the **Pending the secretariat** state. It can arrive by two routes:

- **Resolved by the centre:** the Director has issued the official resolution. The resolution PDF is in the form's **Resolution** field.
- **Resolved by the Ministry:** the Head of Studies has recorded the Ministry's outcome. If they uploaded it, the Ministry's resolution is in the **Resolution** field too.

Each resolved request appears in the activity tray (🕒) of all secretariat staff. When someone registers it, the task disappears from everyone's tray.

---

## Access

Navigate to: **Academic management → Convalidations** and apply the **Pending the secretariat** filter. You can also search a request by its **registration number** (e.g. CONV-2026-27-0001).

To see a student's requests, open their record and click the **Convalidations** button.

---

## Registering a resolution

1. Open the request.
2. Click the file name in the **Resolution** field to open the PDF in a new tab, and check on the **Subjects** tab which modules are convalidated and with which grade.
3. Register the resolution in Esfera.
4. Click **Registered in Esfera** and confirm.

![Request pending the secretariat](../../assets/secretary/convalidations-secretary.png)

The resolution is already official: neither the modules, nor the grades, nor the reasons for refusal can be changed.

When you register it:

- The request becomes **Completed** if any module is convalidated, or **Rejected** if none is.
- The student receives an email with the resolution and the PDF attached. So does the family if the student is a minor or has authorized sharing information with it.
- The student stops taking each convalidated module: their module enrollment is deleted, so they leave its attendance lists and its open grade session, even if the teacher had already graded them.
- The module's teachers and the group's tutor get a **Convalidated subject** activity in their tray (🕒).
- The academic history records the module as passed, with the grade, the **CV** mark and the registration number.

---

## Registering a request received on paper

1. Click **New**.
2. Choose the **Student**, **Study**, **Course** and **Grounds** (for prior studies, also **Studies passed**: at this centre or elsewhere), and write the applicant's comments if there are any. Unlike on the portal, a request registered here can be saved without supporting documents, to ask for them afterwards.
3. On the **Subjects** tab, add one line for each module requested.
4. On the **Supporting documents** tab, upload the documents.
5. Click **Save**.

The request stays **Pending**, waiting for the Head of Studies to review it. Once saved, the student, study, course, grounds and comments can no longer be changed.

You can register a request, and process any request, at any time: the portal's request period (see [Convalidation settings](../admin/convalidation-settings.md)) only limits new requests from students and families.
