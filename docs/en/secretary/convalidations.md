# Convalidations: completing the validated requests

Register in Esfera the convalidations the Head of Studies validated, and complete them in EMS so the student sees the grade.

**Role required:** Secretariat.

---

## When it is your turn

A request reaches the secretariat once the Head of Studies validates it: it moves to **In progress** and already carries, on each convalidated subject, the grade it was resolved with.

Every validated request lands in the activity inbox (🕒) of everyone in the secretariat. Once someone completes or rejects it, the task leaves everyone's inbox.

---

## Access

Go to: **Academic management → Convalidations** and apply the **Pending the secretariat** filter. You can also search a request by its **registration number** (e.g. CONV-2026-27-0001).

To see a student's requests, open their form and click the **Convalidations** button.

---

## Completing a request

1. Open the request and check, on the **Subjects** tab, which subjects are convalidated and with which grade.
2. Register the convalidation in Esfera.
3. If Esfera's grade differs from the one on the form, correct it in the **Grade** column.
4. Click **Complete** and confirm.

Once completed:

- The student (or the family of a minor) gets an email with the resolution and the grade of each subject.
- The student stops taking each convalidated subject: their enrollment in it is deleted, so they leave its attendance lists and the open grade session, even if the teacher had already graded them.
- The subject's teachers and the group's tutor get a **Convalidated subject** activity in their inbox (🕒).
- The academic history records the subject as passed, with its grade, the **CV** mark and the registration number.
- The request is **Completed** and can no longer be changed.

---

## Rejecting a validated request

Click **Reject** and confirm. The request is closed and the student gets the resolution by email. Write the reason on the **Resolution** tab first, so the student reads it.

---

## Asking the student for documentation

Click **Request information**, write what you need and send it. The student gets an email and can answer, attaching the documents, from the portal on that same request.

---

## Registering a request received on paper

1. Click **New**.
2. Pick the **Student**, the **Study** and the **Grounds**.
3. On the **Subjects** tab, add a line for each requested subject.
4. On the **Supporting documents** tab, upload the documents.
5. Click **Save**.

The request stays **Pending**, waiting for the Head of Studies to validate it.

You can register a request, and handle any request, at any time: the portal's request period (see [Convalidations settings](../admin/convalidation-settings.md)) only limits new requests from students and families.
