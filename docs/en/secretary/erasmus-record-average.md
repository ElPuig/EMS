# Erasmus+: typing a record average

The record average of an Erasmus+ application (criterion A of the call) is calculated by EMS from the student's grades. When EMS has no grade for the student (for example, they joined the centre this year), the average of their certificate of previous studies is typed by hand.

**Required role:** Secretariat (also the Erasmus team and the Head of Studies).

---

1. Go to **Erasmus+ > Applications** and open the application.
2. Go to the **Scoring** tab. It appears once the Erasmus team has closed the call.
3. If **Record average** is 0 and the list of modules is empty, type the certificate's average (0 to 10) in **Average from certificate**, and the certificate in **Certificate**.
4. Save. EMS records who typed it and when.

When EMS has grades for the student the field doesn't appear: the average is always EMS's.
