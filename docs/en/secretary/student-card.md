# Printing student cards

EMS generates the student card as a PDF ready to print and cut.

**Required role:** Secretary, Head of Studies or Administrator, for any student. Tutors print their own students' cards, and so can their Seminar Chief, Department Chief and Head of Studies for those students.

---

## What the card shows

![Front and back of a student card](../../assets/secretary/student-card-example.png)

**Front:**

- The centre's logo, name, address and phone, and the current academic year.
- A band split in two. On the left, the student's level in big letters (ESO, BTX, CFGB, CFGM, CFGS, PFI, EFPS), the study's acronym (SMX, DAW...; not for ESO and BTX, where it would repeat the level) and the course year (1, 2, 3, 4) in a white circle, in the level's own colour.
- On the right, whether the student may leave the centre, for the teacher at the door: **Pot sortir del centre** (green) when the student is of age or the family has accepted the *Leave the Centre* authorization for this academic year, **No pot sortir del centre** (red) otherwise. It is worked out when the card is printed: if the student turns 18 or the family accepts the authorization later, print the card again.
- The photo, last names and first name, identity document (DNI or NIE, or the passport when there is none), birth date and IDALU, with its barcode.
- The centre's stamp. Until the administrator uploads it, an empty box is printed instead, to stamp by hand.

**Back:** the logo of the Departament d'Educació i Formació Professional, the centre's details and a note saying the card is personal and non-transferable.

The card is always printed in Catalan, whatever the language of whoever prints it. Its data comes from the student's record: if something is missing (the photo, for instance), fill it in before printing.

---

## Printing the cards of a whole group

1. Go to **Educational Community → Groups** and open the group.
2. In the header, click **Print student cards**. When there are other actions, the button is inside the **Actions** menu.

![Print student cards button on the group's form](../../assets/secretary/student-card-group-form.png)

The PDF has the card of every student whose main group is this one, sorted by last name.

---

## Printing the cards of a few students

1. Go to **Educational Community → Students** and switch to the list view.
2. Select the students.
3. Open the **Actions** menu (the cog) and click **Print student card**.

![Print student card in the students list's Actions menu](../../assets/secretary/student-card-list.png)

Selected contacts that are not students, or students whose card you can't print, are left out. If none is left, a message tells you so.

---

## Printing one student's card

1. Open the student's form.
2. In the header, open the **Actions** menu and click **Print student card**.

![Print student card in the Actions menu of the student's form](../../assets/secretary/student-card-student-form.png)

---

## Printing and cutting

The PDF is A4, with 10 cards per sheet in two columns. Each card is the size of a credit card (85.6 × 54 mm), so it fits the usual card holders.

Each sheet of fronts is followed by its sheet of backs. Print the PDF double-sided, flipping on the long edge, and every back lands right behind its front. Then cut each card along the dashed line.
