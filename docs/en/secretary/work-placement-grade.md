# Work placement (EM) grade

The work placement grade is normally entered by the group tutor, as each student finishes their internship (see the [tutors' manual](../tutors/work-placement-grade.md)). The secretariat has access to the same tool, with **no restriction of group**: it is the way to close the finals that are still pending from previous courses — a student who passed all the learning outcomes of a module but whose final grade could not be computed because the placement had not been done yet.

---

## Where to find it

**Planning and Grading → Grades → Work placement evaluation (EM)**.

Pick the group: the matrix shows one row per student. Type the grade only in the cells of the students who finished the placement (empty cells are never written) and press **Apply changes**. Tick **Grade per module** on a row to grade that student's modules one by one. See the [tutors' manual](../tutors/work-placement-grade.md) for the details of the grid.

Ex-students (withdrawals and alumni) are never listed: they have left the centre and their history is closed.

## Finding the pending finals

**Planning and Grading → Grades → Academic history**, filter **Finals pending placement**. Every line there is a module of a past course waiting for its placement grade. Those are exactly the lines the wizard offers under the **Previous course** source, and it completes their final grade with the weights frozen in the history record.

## A grade below 5

The placement is repeated, never the module: the module stays *Passed*, the grade is kept for the record and the final stays pending until the student does the placement again.
