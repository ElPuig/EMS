# Signing in to EMS with Your Corporate Google Account

This guide explains how to access the school management platform (EMS) using your corporate Google Workspace account (`@elpuig.xeill.net`).

**Required role:** Teacher or ASP staff

---

## Index

1. [What you need](#what-you-need)
2. [How to sign in](#how-to-sign-in)
3. [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting)

---

## What you need

When you joined the school, you received an email at your personal address with your corporate account credentials (`@elpuig.xeill.net`). Your EMS user was created automatically at the same time, already connected to that Google account — **you do not need a separate EMS password**.

If you have not activated your corporate account yet, do that first: sign in at [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com/) with the credentials you received and set your definitive password.

---

## How to sign in

1. Open the EMS address in your browser (the same link included in your welcome email).
2. On the login page, click the **Sign in with Google** button — do **not** fill in the email/password fields.
3. Choose (or enter) your corporate account `@elpuig.xeill.net`.

That's it: you will land on the EMS home page with your teacher permissions.

> **Tip:** if the browser shows several Google accounts, make sure to pick the **corporate** one; a personal Gmail account will not have access.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **"Access Denied" after choosing the Google account:** you probably picked a personal account. Retry and select the `@elpuig.xeill.net` one.
- **You never received the welcome email:** check with the school office (secretariat) that your personal email address is correctly recorded in your file.
- **The Google account works for email but not for EMS:** contact the administration team so they can review your EMS user link.
