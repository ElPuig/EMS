# Attendance: Reviewing Your Check-ins and Requesting Corrections

Every check-in and check-out you make is recorded automatically. This page explains how to review your own records and, if one has the wrong time, how to request a correction.

**Required role:** Teacher

---

## Reviewing Your Attendance

1. Navigate to **Employee Attendances**.
2. The list shows all your check-in/check-out records, most recent first.
3. Click any record to see its details: check-in time, check-out time, and how the time was recorded (kiosk, manual, etc.).

> You only ever see your own records here — this is private to you and to the Head of Studies / Deputy Head of Studies / Director / Administrator, who can see everyone's.

---

## Requesting a Correction

If a check-in or check-out was recorded with the wrong time (you forgot to clock in, a kiosk glitch, etc.):

1. Open the attendance record with the wrong time.
2. Click **Request Correction** in the header.
3. A form opens with the **Check-in** and/or **Check-out** time already filled in with the current value — you only need to change the one(s) that are wrong. Note that you can only correct the *time of day*, not the date.
4. Explain why in the **Reason** field.
5. Click **Save**. The request is now **Pending**.

> Once saved, the reason can no longer be edited — if you need to add more context afterward, use the chatter at the bottom of the request to leave a comment.

---

## What Happens Next

Your request is sent automatically to whoever can validate it — normally your Head of Studies or Deputy Head of Studies (found by walking up the management chain from your own manager; if none is configured, an Administrator is notified instead so they can fix it).

- They review the original time against what you requested, and your reason.
- **Accept**: your check-in/check-out is corrected to the requested time.
- **Reject**: your record is left as it was.
- Either way, you're notified automatically (you'll see it in the chatter of the request, and by email).
- If they made a mistake, they can change their decision afterward — you'll be notified again if that happens.

---

## Checking the Status of Your Requests

- **Employee Attendances → Correction Requests** lists all the requests you've made and their current status (Pending / Accepted / Rejected).
- From the attendance record itself, the **Corrections** button in the header (only visible if a request exists for that record) takes you straight to it.

> If you're also a Head of Studies, Deputy Head of Studies or Director, see the [Head of Studies manual](../head_of_studies/attendance-corrections.md) for how to decide on requests sent to you.
