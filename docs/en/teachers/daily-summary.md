# Daily Summary

This guide explains the daily message with everything EMS has for you (pending tasks, your students' attendance issues if you are a tutor, and notices for your information), when it arrives and how to choose when and how you receive it.

**Role required:** any member of staff with an EMS user

## What it is

EMS doesn't send you an email every time something that isn't urgent happens: it keeps it and sends it all together, **at most once a day**, at the moment you have chosen. Your inbox doesn't fill up and you have a single daily check.

Urgent matters (for example, a guard duty for today) still arrive straight away.

> **Since version 18.0.0.44.0:** the pending-tasks summary and the tutors' attendance issues report are a single message, and it can no longer be turned off: it arrives whenever there is something, by email or in the EMS inbox, as you choose.

## What it contains

![Example of the daily summary: pending tasks grouped by type and a notice about a student](../../assets/teachers/resum-diari-02-correu.png)

Only the sections with something in them are shown:

- **Pending tasks:** every task in your activities tray (🕒), grouped by type (for example, *Review convalidation request*). For each task, a link that opens it in EMS, its description and its due date; overdue ones are marked in brackets. If a type has more than 20 tasks, the 20 due soonest are shown, plus how many more there are.
- **Your students' attendance issues** (tutors only): absences, delays, etc. recorded since the previous summary, by day and student. See [Your Students' Attendance Issues Report](../tutors/attendance-issues-report.md).
- **For your information:** notices that ask nothing of you, grouped by student. For example, that a student of yours has an [expected withdrawal](../tutors/withdrawal-expected.md) and no longer appears in your roll-calls, that they won't attend a subject of yours while their convalidation request is resolved, or that some of their subjects have been convalidated.

The subject line sums it up, for example *EMS: daily summary (pending tasks: 3, notices: 1)*.

## When it arrives

- **At the moment you have chosen** (see below). By default, **at the start of your working day**, according to your schedule; if you have no schedule in EMS, the centre's schedule framework is used.
- **Only if there is something.** With nothing pending and no notice, you get nothing that day.
- **Anything arriving after the chosen moment waits for the next summary.** For example, a task assigned to you at noon reaches you first thing the next working day.
- **Tasks and notices don't arrive on days you don't work** (weekends, public holidays, an approved whole-day absence). Your students' attendance issues don't wait for you to come back: on a day you don't work, they arrive at the end of the centre's day.

---

## Choosing when and how you receive it

1. Click your avatar, at the top right of EMS, and choose **My Profile**.
2. Open the **Preferences** tab.
3. In the **Daily summary** group, choose the moment in **Daily summary** (1) and, if you choose **At a fixed time**, enter the time.
4. In **Notification** (2), choose whether you receive it **by email** or **in EMS** (the inbox at the top). This option applies to every other EMS notification too.
5. Save.

![The Preferences tab of My Profile: the daily summary moment (1) and how you receive notifications (2)](../../assets/teachers/resum-diari-01-preferencies.png)

| Moment | When it arrives |
|--------|-----------------|
| **When my working day starts** (default) | At the start of your working day, according to your schedule. |
| **When my working day ends** | When your working day ends. |
| **When my students' classes end (tutors)** | When the last class of the day of any of your students ends. If you don't tutor a group, it works like *When my working day ends*. |
| **At a fixed time** | At the time you choose, every school day. |

The change also applies to the summary waiting to be sent at that moment.
