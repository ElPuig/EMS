# Accompanying an Erasmus+ group mobility

If you are the **leader teacher** or an **accompanying teacher** of an Erasmus+ group mobility, you will find it in **Erasmus+ > My group mobilities**. You do not apply to any call: the Erasmus coordination adds you.

---

## Leader teacher

- **Selection:** when the enrolment closes, the **Selection** tab shows the ranking EMS calculates: the rank, the record average and whether each student meets the requirements (you never see the conduct warnings nor other confidential data). You cannot change grades or ranks. If you think an exception is needed, write it in *Exception requested by the leader* and click **Request the exception**. When everything is right, in **Actions**, **Send the proposal to the coordinator**. The coordination approves it and publishes the results.
- **Withdrawals:** if a selected student cannot go, the mobility shows the next reserve: in **Actions**, **Propose the next reserve**. The coordination approves it.
- **Documents:** in the **Documents** tab and in each participant's file you can review the documents (mark them valid or as needing changes) and upload the group's shared documents. Confidential documents are only for the Erasmus team.
- **Programme and stay:** write the activity programme and, during the stay, the **follow-up** (attendance, incidents, changes, evidence). Mobility incidents are not conduct warnings.
- **Return:** in **Actions**, **The stay starts** and **Back home**. On return you get the task of answering the **EU Survey**: once answered, click **EU Survey answered** (you can attach evidence). At the end, **Propose the closure**.

## Accompanying teacher

You can see the group, the programme and the participants, write the stay's **follow-up** (attendance, incidents, evidence) and upload your own documents to your file. You do not see the enrolments nor the ranking, and you cannot validate the selection nor change administrative data.
