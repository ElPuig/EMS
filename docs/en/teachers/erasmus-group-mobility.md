# Accompanying an Erasmus+ group mobility

If you are the **leader teacher** or an **accompanying teacher** of an Erasmus+ group mobility, you will find it in **Erasmus+ > My group mobilities**. You do not apply to any call: the Erasmus coordination adds you.

---

## Leader teacher

- **Selection:** when the enrolment closes, the **Selection** tab shows the ranking EMS calculates: the rank, the record average and whether each student meets the requirements (you never see the conduct warnings nor other confidential data). You cannot change grades or ranks. If you think an exception is needed, write it in *Exception requested by the leader* and click **Request the exception**. When everything is right, in **Actions**, **Send the proposal to the coordinator**. The coordination publishes the provisional list (students can claim until the claims deadline) and then approves the selection with the definitive list. If a tie remains or a student has no grades, the proposal cannot be sent until the Erasmus team solves it.
- **Withdrawals:** if a selected student tells you they cannot go, record it with the **Renounce** button of their enrolment in the **Selection** tab, writing the reason (they can also do it from the portal). The mobility then shows the next reserve: in **Actions**, **Propose the next reserve**. The coordination approves it. If that reserve does not want the place, record it the same way with **Renounce** on their enrolment, and propose the next one.
- **Documents:** in the **Documents** tab and in each participant's file you can review the documents (mark them valid or as needing changes) and upload the group's shared documents. A document someone signs is complete only once you mark it valid, after checking its signatures. Confidential documents (for example the European Health Insurance Card) are only for the Erasmus team.
- **Programme and stay:** write the activity programme and, during the stay, the **follow-up** (attendance, incidents, changes, evidence). Mobility incidents are not conduct warnings.
- **Return:** in **Actions**, **The stay starts** and **Back home**. On return you get two tasks. Say whether the **programme was carried out as planned** (**Return and closure** tab): yes, or no and what changed; the team then uploads the executed group learning agreement, signed by both centres. And answer the **EU Survey** for the whole group: once answered, click **EU Survey answered** (you can attach evidence). At the end, **Propose the closure** (it needs the answer about the programme).

## Accompanying teacher

You can see the group, the programme and the participants, write the stay's **follow-up** (attendance, incidents, evidence) and upload your own documents to your file. You do not see the enrolments nor the ranking, and you cannot validate the selection nor change administrative data.
