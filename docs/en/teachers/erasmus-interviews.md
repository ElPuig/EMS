# Scoring an Erasmus+ interview

When the Erasmus team asks you to evaluate a candidate's interview, you score it in EMS with the rubric of the call.

**Who:** any member of the staff assigned as evaluator by the Erasmus team.

---

## 1. Find your interviews

When you are assigned an interview you receive a task in EMS. When the Erasmus team schedules many interviews at once, you get a single notice with the whole list (date and time, place and candidate) instead of one email per interview. The **Erasmus+** menu appears, with **My evaluations**: the interviews assigned to you, with the candidate, the date and the place.

The list shows the totals once you have submitted each interview. You only see your own interviews. You don't see the other evaluator's score nor the rest of the candidate's application.

## 2. Score the rubric

Open the interview and score each criterion from 0 to 5. The descriptors of the rubric (annex 4 of the call) are written under each criterion, so you can score during the interview; an intermediate score is fine when the answer is between two levels.

| Part | Criteria | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| General | Motivation; maturity, autonomy and money management; handling difficulties; adaptation and communication | 20 |
| English | Comprehension; fluency and interaction; accuracy, vocabulary and pronunciation | 15 |

You can write your own notes in **Evaluator's notes**.

## 3. Submit it

Click **Actions > Submit** once every criterion is scored. The task is marked as done. **Actions > Submit and next** submits it and opens the next interview you still have to score.

Until the call's **score correction deadline** you can **Reopen** it and change it. After the deadline it can no longer be changed: if there is a mistake, tell the Erasmus team.
