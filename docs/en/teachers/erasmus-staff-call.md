# Applying for an Erasmus+ staff mobility

Apply for a job shadowing, a structured course or a teaching assignment abroad, and follow your application, from the school portal.

**Who:** vocational training teachers (basic, intermediate and higher training cycles, specialisation courses and PFI), the Director and the Head of Studies of vocational training. Only they see the **Erasmus+** card on the portal.

---

## 1. Open the call

Go to **Erasmus+** on the school portal (`/my/erasmus`; the Erasmus team shares the link when the call opens). You see the staff call and its dates. Click **Read the call** to download the published call, and **Apply** to open the form.

## 2. Fill in the form

- **Activity:** job shadowing, structured course or teaching assignment.
- **Host entity or course** and **Destination** (city and country): always a concrete one, with its name. You will find options on the [centre's Erasmus+ website](https://elpuig.xeill.net/el-centre/erasmus/recursos-i-formacio).
- **Preferred dates or period** and the approximate duration: preferably in the non-teaching period (June, July); at another time, such as April, only a week that does not affect the assessments, and with the Direction's validation.
- The **month and year of your last Erasmus+ staff mobility** from the centre, or tick that you never had one. Accompanying students, other programmes and mobilities from other centres don't count.
- The **month and year since when you work at the centre** without interruption.
- The **centre projects**, commissions or coordinations you take part in (last course and this one).
- Your highest official **language certificate** (B2 or higher), if you have one. Attach it in the documents too.
- Optional: the **support** you need to do the mobility. This answer is confidential: only the Erasmus team reads it.
- The voluntary consent to use your image.
- The **mobility and transfer plan** (annex 1), the only part of your application the panel reads: why this entity or course, two or three objectives and how you will apply it to your job, 1,500 characters at most each. Together with the activity, host, destination and dates, every section is required.

**Dissemination** is not part of the plan: it is the same for everyone. When you come back, you publish a news item on the centre's website about your experience, following the Erasmus+ visual identity and dissemination policy, and, if it is considered necessary, you share it with your colleagues at a department or cycles meeting.

Attach the documents the call asks for: identity document and Europass CV in English. The language certificates are optional.

You can't submit it without your last mobility and the date you joined the centre. Click **Save draft** to keep working on it later, or **Submit application** when everything is ready. You receive a receipt by email. Until the closing date you can change it and submit it again.

## 3. Follow it

On the same page you see the state of your application, the corrections the Erasmus team asks for (if any) and, once published, each resolution with your position and the points of every criterion. The published lists show only four digits of the identity document, never names.

If you are selected, **accept or renounce the place** on the portal before the deadline: without an answer, the place is considered renounced.

## 4. Prepare your mobility

When you accept the place, the page shows **Your mobility** with your proposal, filled in from your application. Complete it with the **exact start and end dates**, accept the responsible declaration for your absence for training and click **Send the proposal**: the Direction (the Director or the Head of Studies of FP) validates the activity, the host entity or course, the destination and the dates. When it does, EMS requests your absence for training for those days on its own, and whoever approves your absences approves it as usual. If it is not validated, you will receive the reason by email: open **Propose another activity**, change what is needed and send it again.

**If it changes later:** once validated, if the course, the host entity or the dates change (for example, the course is not offered that month), open **Change the activity or the dates** in **Your mobility** before it starts and send the new proposal. The Direction validates it again, and until then your mobility stays as it was validated. If the Direction leaves a comment, you see it in the email and on the portal.

Once validated, the card shows the documents to hand in before leaving and on return (European Health Insurance Card, grant agreement, learning agreement signed by you, the Erasmus coordinator and the host entity, on return the Europass mobility document (FP staff, KA121-VET) or the certificate of stay or attendance (higher education, KA131-HED), a short report with evidence within a month of your return...), who signs each one and its deadline, and the steps to follow (preparation meetings, activities left for your groups, final report, news item on the website and, if needed, sharing it at a meeting). The Erasmus team uploads the grant agreement filled in (download it, sign it and upload it signed) and your insurance policy (download it). Sign the other documents outside EMS, attach them and click **Upload the documents**.
