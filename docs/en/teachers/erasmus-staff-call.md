# Applying for an Erasmus+ staff mobility

Apply for a job shadowing, a structured course or a teaching assignment abroad, and follow your application, from the school portal.

**Who:** vocational training staff (teachers, administration and services staff, management team).

---

## 1. Open the call

Go to **Erasmus+** on the school portal (`/my/erasmus`; the Erasmus team shares the link when the call opens). You see the staff call and its dates. Click **Read the call** to download the published call, and **Apply** to open the form.

## 2. Fill in the form

- **Activity:** job shadowing, structured course or teaching assignment.
- **Host entity or course** and **Destination** (city and country). If you have not found one yet, tick the box and the Erasmus coordination will help you.
- **Preferred dates or period** and the approximate duration.
- The **month and year of your last Erasmus+ staff mobility** from the centre, or tick that you never had one. Accompanying students, other programmes and mobilities from other centres don't count.
- The **month and year since when you work at the centre** without interruption.
- The **centre projects**, commissions or coordinations you take part in (last course and this one).
- Your highest official **language certificate** (B2 or higher), if you have one. Attach it in the documents too.
- Optional: whether you want to accompany groups of students, and the **support** you need to do the mobility. This answer is confidential: only the Erasmus team reads it.
- The voluntary consent to use your image.

Attach the documents the call asks for: identity document, Europass CV in English and the **mobility plan** (annex 1, two pages at most). The language certificates are optional.

You can't submit it without your last mobility and the date you joined the centre. Click **Save draft** to keep working on it later, or **Submit application** when everything is ready. You receive a receipt by email. Until the closing date you can change it and submit it again.

## 3. Follow it

On the same page you see the state of your application, the corrections the Erasmus team asks for (if any) and, once published, each resolution with your position and the points of every criterion. The published lists show only four digits of the identity document, never names.

If you are selected, **accept or renounce the place** on the portal before the deadline: without an answer, the place is considered renounced.

## 4. Prepare your mobility

When you accept the place, the page shows **Your mobility** with your proposal, filled in from your application. Complete it with the **exact start and end dates** and click **Send the proposal**: the Direction (the Director or the Head of Studies) validates the activity, the host entity or course, the destination and the dates. If it is not validated, you will receive the reason by email: open **Propose another activity**, change what is needed and send it again.

Once validated, the card shows the documents to hand in before leaving and on return (European Health Insurance Card, grant agreement, mobility programme signed by you, the centre and the host entity, certificate of attendance, a short report with evidence within a month of your return...), who signs each one and the deadlines, and the steps to follow (preparation meetings, activities left for your groups, final report, dissemination session). Sign the documents outside EMS, attach them and click **Upload the documents**. Then request your **absence for training** for the validated dates: accept the responsible declaration and click **Request the absence**. Whoever approves your absences approves it as usual.
