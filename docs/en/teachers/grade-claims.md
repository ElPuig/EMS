# Grade claims: answering them

When an evaluation is **published**, students (or the family of a minor) can claim a grade from the portal during the claims period. A claim is not a request for a higher grade: the student claims a grade when it **does not match the calculation you shared** (a mark that was left out, something that was not taken into account) and explains what does not match.

The **teacher** of the subject answers first. If the teacher denies it, the student can take it to their **tutor**, who answers in a second instance. The **Head of Studies** of the tutor's area can answer either.

## Where you find them

**Planning and Grading → Grades → Grade claims**

The list opens with the **Pending** filter. A new claim is also a task in your daily summary until you answer it.

You see the claims of your subjects and, if you are a tutor, those of the students you tutor.

---

## Answering a claim

1. Open the claim. It shows the student, the subject, the learning outcome (or the final grade), the grade when it was claimed and **what does not match**, in the student's words.
2. Open **Actions** and choose:
   - **Accept**: type the **Corrected grade** and press **Confirm**. EMS writes it in the evaluation session; nothing else of the session can be modified. For a claim about the **final grade**, the corrected grade replaces the subject's centre grade (*Override Internal*).
   - **Deny**: choose the **Reason** and, if you want, add **Details**. The reason **Others** requires details. Press **Confirm**.

Claims can be answered while the evaluation is published. The Head of Studies cannot finalise the evaluation while any claim is unanswered.

---

## Second instance: the tutor

When the teacher denies a claim, the student can take it to the tutor from the portal, explaining why, until the end of the claims period (or two school days after the denial, if that is later). The tutor then gets the task and answers it the same way: **Accept** with the corrected grade or **Deny** with a reason. The tutor's answer closes the claim.

---

## What the student receives

The student (and the family of a minor) gets an email each time a claim is answered, with the decision and, if it is denied, the reason. After the teacher's denial, the email also says until when the claim can be taken to the tutor. The portal shows the state of each claim and the answers.
