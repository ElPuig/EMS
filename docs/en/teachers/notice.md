# Notices: Writing to Your Department's Groups

This page covers **Communications → Notices** for Department Chiefs and Seminar Chiefs. The screen and the create/send workflow are the same ones described in the [Administrator manual](../admin/notice.md#creating-and-sending-a-notice); this page only covers what is different for you.

**Required role:** Department Chief / Seminar Chief

---

## Which Groups You Can Write To

You can send notices to the students and families of the **groups your department teaches**: every group where a teacher of your department (or of one of its sub-departments) has classes, according to the teachers' schedules. The **Groups** field only offers those. There is nothing to configure: when a teacher's schedule changes, the groups you can reach change with it.

---

## Which Notices You See

The list opens with the **"Show only mine"** filter, showing the notices you created. Remove it to also see the notices other people have sent to your department's groups. You can read those, but you can only edit, send or delete your own and those of the chiefs below you in the chain of command (for example, the Seminar Chiefs who depend on you). The Head of Studies above you can also review and send yours.

---

## Timetable Changes Because of an Absence

When a teacher of your department is away, the guard duty schedule can send a notice telling a group's students and families they can come in later, leave earlier or have no classes, or its students alone that their break is longer (those under 18 still cannot leave the centre). Whether you send it from the board (**Send notice**) or accept a rearrangement of the lessons (one notice per group, with every change of its day), it is sent straight away, and only opens here as a draft addressed to that group if it could not be sent; its groups cannot be changed. See [Organising an absence](guard-duty-schedule.md#organising-an-absence).

When you rearrange the lessons instead (a swap or a moved lesson), the board also sends a notice to the group's students only, listing the lessons that change time that day; it only opens here as a draft if it could not be sent, or as a correction to review if you remove an arrangement after it was sent. Its groups and its recipients cannot be changed. See [Rearranging the lessons instead of sending a guard](guard-duty-schedule.md#rearranging-the-lessons-instead-of-sending-a-guard).
