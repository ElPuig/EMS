# Plannings: checking your subjects' grading ponderations

This guide explains the **Plannings** screen: where the internal/work-placement split and the
per-learning-outcome (RA) weighting used to calculate your students' grades comes from.

## Access

**Planning and Grading → Plannings**

## What a planning is

A planning is the grading configuration of one subject for one specific academic year: how much
of the final grade comes from the centre's own assessment (internal) versus a work placement
(external), and, within the internal part, how much weight each learning outcome (RA) carries.
This is exactly what [Entering grades by learning outcome](qualificacions.md#how-the-grade-is-
computed) uses to compute the internal grade and the final grade of each module.

A planning belongs to a specific course — the weights that applied last year are not
necessarily the ones in force this year, and a correction to an old, already-closed course
always uses the weights that were actually in force *then*. The course a planning belongs to is
shown (read-only) as the first field of its form.

## What you can see

The list defaults to **Show only mine**, showing the plannings of the subjects you personally
teach. Remove the filter's facet from the search bar and you'll see... nothing new: this screen
only ever shows the subjects you teach, regardless of the filter, since that's what your own
access is scoped to.

It also defaults to **Show only current course**. Remove that facet to see the plannings of
every academic year (for example, to check the weights a past course's grades were calculated
with).

Opening a planning shows its internal/external split and, in the **Outcome ponderation** tab,
each learning outcome's own share of the internal part. The history panel at the side of the form
records every change made to the planning (who changed what, and when).

## Requesting a change

Plannings are **read-only** for teachers — you can consult your own subjects' ponderations here,
but creating or editing one is done by the academic administration or Head of Studies/Deputy. If
a weight needs correcting (e.g. after a new learning outcome is added to a module), let them know
directly.
