# Reserving a space

Use **Educational Community → Space reservations** to hold a classroom, lab or any other space of the centre for a time, for example a talk, a make-up class or a meeting with families.

## What counts as taken

A space is taken when it is in someone's weekly timetable at that time (a class or a duty), when somebody has already reserved it, or when a meeting is held in it. The meeting minute's **Room** counts as a reservation: you do not have to reserve it twice.

## Reserve a space

1. Open **Educational Community → Space reservations**. It opens on the calendar; you can also switch to the list.
2. Press **New**.
3. Fill in **Start** and **Duration**. The **Space** field then only offers the spaces that are free for that time, so there is nothing to check by hand.
4. Write the **Reason** and save.

If you change the time afterwards and the space is no longer free, EMS refuses to save and tells you who has it and when.

## Your reservations

The **My reservations** filter shows only yours. You can change or delete your own reservations; you cannot change other people's. The secretariat and the administrators can change any of them.

A reservation made by a meeting's minute is read-only here: change the room, the date or the duration in the minute itself and the reservation follows. If you take the room out, or the meeting becomes online, the reservation disappears.
