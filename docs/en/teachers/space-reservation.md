# Reserving a space

Use **Educational Community → Spaces → Space reservations** to hold a classroom, lab or any other space of the centre for a time, for example a talk, a make-up class or a meeting with families.

## What counts as taken

A space is taken when it is in someone's weekly timetable at that time (a class or a duty), when somebody has already reserved it, or when a meeting is held in it. The meeting minute's **Room** counts as a reservation: you do not have to reserve it twice. Spaces marked as not reservable (services...) are never offered.

## Reserve a space

Press **New** on the list of reservations. A window opens: enter the **day**, the **start** and the **duration**, then choose how to find the space.

- **I need a free space:** leave **Space** empty (you can narrow it by **Type**) and press **Search free spaces**. The window lists the spaces that are free at that time. Write a **reason** and press **Reserve** on the one you want.
- **I need this space:** choose it in **Space**. The window tells you whether it is free at that time and lists everything that holds it that day, classes of the students included, with the hours. If it is free, write a **reason** and press **Reserve**.

You cannot reserve a time that has already started. If you change the day, the time or the duration after searching, search again.

## Your reservations

The list shows all the reservations; the **My reservations** filter shows only yours. Open one to change or delete it: you can change your own, not other people's. If you change the time and the space is no longer free, EMS refuses to save and tells you who has it and when. The secretariat and the administrators can change any of them.

A reservation made by a meeting's minute is read-only here: change the room, the date or the duration in the minute itself and the reservation follows. If you take the room out, or the meeting becomes online, the reservation disappears.
