# The Presence Dot on the Teachers Screen

This guide explains what the coloured dot next to each person's name on the **Teachers** and **ASP** screens means.

**Required role:** Teacher (or any employee with an EMS user account)

---

## Index

1. [Where to see it](#where-to-see-it)
2. [What each colour means](#what-each-colour-means)
3. [What it is based on](#what-it-is-based-on)

---

## Where to see it

**Educational Community > Teachers** (or **ASP**): every card shows a small dot next to the person's name. It also appears at the top of their record when you open it.

---

## What each colour means

| Dot | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| Green | **Present**: the person has checked in. |
| Yellow | **Absent**: according to their schedule they should be working right now (one of their slots has started and not yet ended), but they haven't checked in. |
| Grey | **Out of working hours**: their schedule has no slot right now - before their first slot of the day, in a gap between two slots, after their last one, or on a day they don't work. |
| Plane | **On leave**: the person has an approved absence today. |

---

## What it is based on

Only two things count: the **check-in/check-out** and the person's own **weekly schedule**.

- Having EMS open in a browser does **not** make someone appear as present: only checking in does.
- Someone whose first class starts at 10:00 appears as out of working hours until 10:00, not as absent.
- The dot shows the situation at the moment the screen was loaded: reload the page to see the current one.
