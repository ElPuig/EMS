# Checking a student card

The back of every student card has a QR code. Scanning it shows whether the card is genuine and whether the student may leave the centre right now. You don't need to sign in to EMS.

**Required role:** anyone holding the card. No EMS account is needed.

---

## Checking a card

1. Scan the QR on the back of the card with your phone's camera.
2. Open the link that appears.
3. Compare what the page shows with what is printed on the card.

![Check page of a valid card](../../assets/teachers/student-card-check-valid.png)

The page shows:

- The level, the study and the course year, as on the card.
- **Pot sortir del centre** (green) or **No pot sortir del centre** (red), as it is right now, even if it changed after the card was printed.
- The student's initials and the last four digits of their IDALU. They must match the name and the IDALU printed on the card.
- **Carnet vàlid**, with the date and time of the check and a blinking dot. They show it is the page itself and not a screenshot.

If the level, the course, the initials or the IDALU don't match the card, the card has been tampered with.

---

## A card that is not valid

![Check page of a card that is not valid](../../assets/teachers/student-card-check-invalid.png)

The page shows **Carnet no vàlid** and no data when the card has been revoked (lost or stolen), belongs to a previous academic year, or belongs to a student who no longer studies at the centre. Hold on to the card and take it to the secretary's office.
