# Students: your own groups

This guide explains how the student list is filtered when you open it, and how to widen it.

**Required role:** Teacher

## What you see when you open Students

Open **Educational Community → Students**. Two filters are already applied in the search bar:

- **Students** — hides former students (alumni, withdrawals and expulsions).
- **My students** — shows only the students of your own groups.

Your own groups are the ones on your timetable plus any group you are the tutor of. A student
is listed when that group is their main group, or when they are enrolled in one of your
subjects in it — reinforcement groups and *desdobles* included.

![The Students list with both filters applied, showing only a student of the teacher's own group](../../assets/teachers/alumnat-01-els-meus-grups.png)

---

## Seeing every student

Click the **×** on the **My students** filter. The list then shows every student in the centre.

Remove the **Students** filter the same way to also include alumni, withdrawals and expulsions.

---

## Going back to your own groups

Open the **Filters** menu in the search bar and click **My students** again. Opening the
**Students** menu entry afresh also restores both filters.
