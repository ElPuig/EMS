# Daily Summary of Pending Tasks

This guide explains the email EMS sends you every morning summarising your pending tasks, and how to turn it off.

**Required role:** any staff member with an EMS user account

---

1. [What it is](#what-it-is)

---

## What it is

Many EMS procedures reach you as a **task** in the activities tray (🕒) at the top of the screen: a convalidation request to review, an absence supporting document to validate, an attendance correction, etc. Many of these tasks, such as convalidation ones, send no email when they are created, so your inbox doesn't fill up with one message per task.

So that nothing slips past you on a day you couldn't open EMS, every working day you get **a single email** listing all your pending tasks. It is not an urgency alert: it only reminds you of what is in your tray.

---

## When it arrives

- **At the start of your working day**, according to your schedule. If you have no schedule in EMS, the school's schedule framework is used.
- **Only on days you work:** not at weekends, on public holidays, or on days covered entirely by an approved absence.
- **Only if you have at least one pending task.** If your tray is empty, you get nothing that day.
- At most one a day.

---

## What it contains

![Example of the daily summary email: tasks grouped by type, with a link, due date and overdue ones marked](../../assets/teachers/resum-tasques-02-correu.png)

- How many tasks you have pending, also in the subject line.
- The tasks grouped by type (for example, *Review convalidation request*), with how many there are of each.
- For each task: a link that opens the record in EMS directly, its description and its due date. Overdue tasks are marked in brackets.
- If a type has more than 20 tasks, the 20 due soonest are listed, followed by how many more there are.

To resolve a task, open it from the link or from the 🕒 tray in EMS. Tasks you have already closed don't show up again the next day.

---

## Turning it off

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner of EMS and choose **My Profile**.
2. Open the **Preferences** tab.
3. Under **Notifications**, untick **Daily summary of pending tasks**.
4. Save.

![My Profile's Preferences tab, with the "Daily summary of pending tasks" option (1) under Notifications](../../assets/teachers/resum-tasques-01-preferencies.png)

To get it again, tick the same option.
