# Academic history of your students

Every student keeps a permanent **academic history**: one record per course with the study, group, subjects taken (grades per learning outcome, round by round) and attendance. As a tutor you can consult the history of the **students of your tutored group** — useful to see at a glance where a student comes from, which subjects they carry over and whether they repeated a course.

---

## Where to find it

Open the student's form and go to the **Academic history** tab. Each line is one course; open it to see the subjects and, inside each subject, the grades of every learning outcome (RA) per round.

The records are ordered **by study and course**, so you can read each study from start to end. A repeated course appears naturally as two consecutive records of the same study and level.

## How to read a record

- **Academic result:** *Fully passed*, *Partially passed* (promotes carrying some subject), *Repeating* or *Withdrawn*.
- **Title obtained:** marked when the student got the title that course.
- **Subject state:** **Passed / Not passed**, decided **only by the RAs**. A subject with every RA passed is passed even if the work placement (EM) is still pending: in that case the **final grade** stays empty (*Final pending*) until the placement is graded. A failed placement is repeated — it never fails the subject.
- **Round columns:** each RA shows the grade standing at each round; the resolved grade is the one of the last round in which it was evaluated.
- **Frozen weights:** the grading weights (internal/EM and per-RA) are the ones in force that course, stored with the record — the history stays valid even if the teaching plan changes later.

> The history is **read-only**: it is a frozen copy of what the grades subsystem computed during the course. If something needs correcting, contact the secretariat or the administrator.
