# Your Students' Attendance Issues Report

This guide explains the email EMS sends you, as a group tutor, with your students' attendance issues (absences, delays...), and how to choose when you receive it.

**Required role:** group tutor (only shown to teachers who tutor a group)

---

1. [What it contains](#what-it-contains)
2. [Choosing when you receive it](#choosing-when-you-receive-it)
3. [Which option to choose](#which-option-to-choose)

---

## What it contains

Every time a teacher records an attendance issue for one of your students in a roll-call (an absence, a delay, etc.), EMS keeps it for your report. The email lists them **by day and by student**: subject, time and status, plus the teacher's notes if there are any. An issue corrected afterwards (for example, an absence that turned out to be a delay) appears crossed out, with the correction after it.

**Each report includes every issue recorded since the previous one.** Nothing gets lost whatever moment you choose: if a teacher takes a roll-call late, after your report has already gone out, that issue arrives in the next one.

If there is nothing new, you get no email.

---

## Choosing when you receive it

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner of EMS and choose **My Profile**.
2. Open the **Preferences** tab.
3. Under **Notifications**, choose an option in **Attendance issues report**.
4. If you choose **At a fixed time**, enter the time in **Attendance issues report time**.
5. Save.

The change also applies to the report you have pending at that moment.

---

## Which option to choose

| Option | When it arrives | Good for you if... |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------|
| **When my working day ends** (default) | When your own working day ends, according to your schedule. | You want to read it before leaving. If your students still have classes afterwards, those issues arrive in the next report. |
| **When my students' day ends** | When the last class of any of your students ends that day, according to each student's own schedule (a subject taken in another group counts too). | You want the whole day in a single email, as soon as possible. |
| **When my next working day starts** | When your next working day starts, according to your schedule. | You prefer to review it when you arrive, with the previous day complete. |
| **At a fixed time** | At the time you choose, every school day. | You have your own routine (for example, 21:20). |

If EMS can't work out the chosen moment on a given day (for example, you have no schedule, or you are on leave for a long time), the report arrives at the end of the school day.
