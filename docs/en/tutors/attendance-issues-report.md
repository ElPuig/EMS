# Your Students' Attendance Issues Report

This guide explains how you receive, as a group tutor, your students' attendance issues (absences, delays...) and how to choose when.

**Role required:** group tutor

---

## Where they arrive

Your students' issues are the **Your students' attendance issues** section of your [daily summary](../teachers/daily-summary.md), the same message that brings your pending tasks and the notices for your information.

> **Since version 18.0.0.44.0:** the attendance issues report is no longer a separate email; if you had kept the default moment (when your working day ends), it now reaches you at the start of your working day, with the daily summary.

---

## What it shows

Every time a teacher records an attendance issue of one of your students in a roll-call (an absence, a delay, etc.), EMS keeps it for your summary. The section lists them **by day and student**: subject, time and status, plus the teacher's notes if any. An issue corrected afterwards (for example, an absence that was really a delay) appears struck through, followed by the correction.

**Each summary includes every issue recorded since the previous one.** Nothing is lost whatever moment you choose: if a teacher takes a roll-call late, after your summary went out, that issue arrives in the next one.

Absences that appear when an [expected withdrawal](withdrawal-expected.md) is reverted, or when a subject the student chose not to attend while their convalidation was resolved is refused, only reach you, not the family.

---

## Choosing when

The moment is your daily summary's: **My Profile** > **Preferences** tab > **Daily summary** group. See [Choosing when and how you receive it](../teachers/daily-summary.md#choosing-when-and-how-you-receive-it).

| Option | Suits you if... |
|--------|-----------------|
| **When my working day starts** (default) | You prefer to check it on arrival, with the previous day complete. |
| **When my working day ends** | You want to read it before leaving. If your students still have classes afterwards, those issues arrive in the next summary. |
| **When my students' classes end (tutors)** | You want the whole day in one message, as soon as possible (a subject taken in another group counts too). |
| **At a fixed time** | You have your own routine (for example, 21:20). |

Issues don't wait for you to come back: on a day you don't work, or when EMS can't work out the chosen moment (for example, you are on long leave), they arrive at the end of the centre's day.
