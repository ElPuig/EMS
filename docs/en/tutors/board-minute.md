# Evaluation board minute

When the Head of Studies moves your group's evaluation sessions to **Board**, EMS creates the minute of that evaluation board for you, already filled in with what it can read from EMS. You complete it during or after the board and send it for approval, as any other minute.

## Where you find it

**Meetings → Minutes and records**: the minute **Acta de junta d'avaluació** of your group, with the evaluation it is about. It is also a task in your daily summary until you send it for approval.

---

## What it already contains

- **Attendance**: the group's teaching team, as in a teaching team minute.
- **Group difficulties**: the four areas (A. Prior knowledge and basic competences, B. Expectations and interests, C. Study techniques and habits, D. Behaviour), for you to fill in.
- **Statistics**, calculated over the term (from the previous board, or from the start of the course): the students who withdrew, with the reason registered; how many students missed more than 25% of their hours; and how many have unjustified absences above 5% of their hours. They are calculated once, when the minute is created: correct them if needed.
- **Students**: every student you tutor, with the **Outcomes passed** and **Outcomes evaluated** in this evaluation, from the grades.

---

## Completing it

1. In **Group difficulties**, write for each area the **Tests** that detected it, the **Group result or diagnosis**, the **Teaching team's objectives** and the **Priority**.
2. In **Students**, tick **Works** for the students who work (the statistics count them) and write:
   - **Teaching team's comments**: what the board says about the student. They stay in the minute and are never shown to the student or the family.
   - **Tutor's comment for the family**: your general comment for the student and the family.
3. If grades changed during the board, press **Update the outcomes from the grades**.
4. Add the agreements and the follow-up of earlier ones, as in any minute, and press **Send for approval**.

---

## What the families see

The **Tutor's comment for the family** appears in the portal, under that evaluation's grades table (and in the grades' PDF), once the evaluation's grades are **published**. Nothing else of the minute is shown to them.
