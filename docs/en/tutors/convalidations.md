# Convalidations: following your students' requests

A student can ask for some subjects of their study to be convalidated. As a tutor, you can **read** the requests of the students you tutor, as the student sees them on the portal, so you can answer their questions. You cannot change them: the Head of Studies reviews them, the Director resolves them and the secretariat registers them.

**Required role:** Tutor

---

## Where to find them

- **From the student's form:** the **Convalidations** button at the top of the form always appears for your students, showing how many requests they have (0 if none). Click it to see them.
- **From the menu:** **Academic management → Convalidations** lists the requests of your students. It opens with the **Pending for me** filter, which shows the requests still in process; remove it to see the finished ones as well.

## What you see on a request

Open a request: the **Request status** tab shows exactly what the student sees on the portal.

- The step the request has reached: pending review, pending documentation, in process at the Ministry, pending the Director or pending the secretariat.
- The subjects requested and, if the student was asked for more documentation, what was asked for.
- The supporting documents the student attached.

While the request is in process, **you do not see what has been decided** for each subject, just as the student does not: until the secretariat registers the resolution, any decision can still change. When the request is completed or rejected, the tab shows the outcome of each subject, the grade and the remarks, the same as the student's portal.

> The grades and the resolution of a request are never shown before it is official, not even if you open it by another route.
