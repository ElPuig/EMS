# Transferring a Student's Tutorship

A student enrolled in 2nd year with 1st-year subjects pending sometimes drops every 2nd-year subject and attends only 1st. The secretary's office (or the Head of Studies) can then transfer their tutorship to the 1st-year group: the student stays in their 2nd-year group in Esfer@, but you, as the 1st-year tutor, take over the follow-up.

**Required role:** Tutor

---

## If you are the new tutor (the group the tutorship was transferred to)

- The student appears among your own students (**My students**), and you manage their data and their family's as with any student of your group.
- You receive their attendance issues in your daily summary, strike notices and the rest of the tutor notices.
- They take part in your group's student walkouts.
- On the **Studies** tab of their form, **Tutorship transferred to** shows your group, **Tutor** shows you and **Official tutor (Esfer@)** shows the tutor of their main group. You can't change the transfer yourself: ask the secretary's office.

## If you are the official tutor (the student's main group)

You keep entering their grades: the evaluation sessions and the academic record go by group, and the student is still in yours. You no longer manage their data nor receive notices about them: that is now the new tutor's job.

The transfer ends by itself when the course changes.
